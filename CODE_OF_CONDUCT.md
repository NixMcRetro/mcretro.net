# Code of Conduct

## Welcome

McRetro.net is a personal website and historical archive documenting retro computing, digital preservation, hardware repairs, software archaeology and the history of the internet.

I welcome constructive contributions, technical discussions and historical corrections from people who share an interest in preserving and understanding technology.

The aim is to maintain an environment where people can exchange knowledge, ask questions and contribute without experiencing hostility or discrimination.

## Expected Behaviour

Everyone participating in this project is expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy and respect.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experiences and levels of technical knowledge.
- Offer constructive criticism and remain open to corrections.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the subject rather than the individual.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks and the disclosure of private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Historical Material

McRetro.net preserves material from different periods of computing and internet history. Some archived content may reflect the language, attitudes or conventions of its original time.

Preservation does not imply endorsement.

Historical material should be presented with appropriate context rather than silently rewritten or removed. Where necessary, explanatory notes or corrections may be added while preserving the integrity of the original record.

This approach does not permit harassment or discriminatory behaviour in present-day project interactions.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact me privately using the contact information on the [McRetro.net About page](https://mcretro.net/about/).

Reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled with appropriate consideration for privacy and confidentiality. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to address a concern.

## Enforcement

I reserve the right to moderate discussions, decline contributions, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation where necessary.

Any action taken will consider the circumstances, severity and impact of the behaviour involved.

## Project Ownership

McRetro.net is an independently maintained personal website and historical archive.

Contributions and feedback are appreciated, but final responsibility for editorial decisions, project direction and moderation remains with the project owner.

## Closing

McRetro.net exists to document, investigate and preserve the technology and stories that might otherwise be forgotten.

Thank you for helping make that possible.
