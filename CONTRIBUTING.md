# Contributing to McRetro.net

Thanks for taking an interest in McRetro.net!

This repository contains the source of my personal website, blog and historical archive. While contributions are welcome, it is important to remember that the material documents real experiences, experiments and investigations across many years.

The aim is to improve accuracy, accessibility and preservation without rewriting history.

## Contributions I Welcome

Useful contributions include:

- Corrections to technical or historical information, preferably with supporting sources.
- Replacement links for references that have disappeared.
- Identification of missing or incorrectly attributed material.
- Improvements to website accessibility, compatibility and performance.
- Fixes for HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Jekyll problems.
- Additional context that improves the usefulness of historical articles.

## Preserving the Original Material

Please do not rewrite old articles as though I knew something at the time that I only discovered years later.

Original publication dates, photographs, videos, historical context and the character of the original writing should be preserved.

Where appropriate, new information can be introduced as a clearly identified later update.

Existing images and media must not be deleted, replaced or silently removed. If a change requires moving or renaming a file, the original material must remain accounted for and existing references must be checked.

## Writing Style

McRetro.net uses first-person Australian English and follows an established editorial style.

Please preserve the author's voice, humour and perspective. Avoid unnecessary changes to wording, artificial narration, generic AI-style language and changes that make an article sound unlike its author.

Use Title Case for article headings, keep paragraphs coherent, and avoid em dashes and en dashes.

Technical terminology, units and identifiers should remain accurate and consistent.

## AI-Assisted Contributions

AI-assisted research and editing are not automatically prohibited, but all contributions must be reviewed for accuracy, historical context and suitability.

Do not submit large automated rewrites, invented technical explanations or unverified information.

AI-generated material should be identified where appropriate.

## Pull Requests

Keep changes focused and explain what you changed and why.

For historical corrections, provide references wherever possible. For website changes, explain how the result was tested.

Please confirm whether any images, videos or other media files were changed, moved or removed.

A pull request does not guarantee acceptance. I retain final editorial and technical control over McRetro.net.

## Copyright and Third-Party Material

The repository contains material with different origins and copyright arrangements.

Do not submit copyrighted third-party material without appropriate permission or a valid legal basis. Please identify the source and applicable permissions for any material you contribute.

No repository-wide licence is currently declared. See the README for further information.

## Thanks!

A surprising amount of the history documented here survives because somebody kept a copy, shared a technical detail or bothered to investigate something obscure.

If you can help preserve another little piece of it, that's always appreciated.
