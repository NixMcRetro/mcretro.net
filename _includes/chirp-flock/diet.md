We love a varied diet of fresh fruits, vegetables, pellets, and seeds. Here's some of our favourites.

Banana, blueberries, strawberry, apple, diced beans (fresh, not frozen), corn (cob and frozen), carrot (not frozen), pellets, seeds (as a treat for when out of their cage, peanuts (Tillie), almonds (Chip), millet, dried pine cones (not technically food).

If changing their diet from the existing pellets or a change of brand it is recommended to introduce this slowly over several weeks. They love soggy pellets as a treat. Be warned, lots of fruit ensures runny poops. Stick to veggies and pellets.

And always provide fresh water!
