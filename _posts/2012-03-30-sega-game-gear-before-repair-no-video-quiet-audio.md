Here's the little Game Gear prior to repair. As you can see from the above it has very quiet sound and no video, overall not bad condition on the inside of the unit. Failing electrolytic capacitors are a common cause of low audio and display problems in ageing Game Gears, so replacing the capacitors was the obvious next diagnostic step.
