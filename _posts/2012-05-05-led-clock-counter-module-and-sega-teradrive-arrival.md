In other news, the Sega TeraDrive Model 2 has arrived from Japan and photos are now in the [Photo Gallery](/goodies).

Sega's own specifications list the Model 2 with 1 MB of RAM and two 3.5-inch floppy drives, but no internal hard drive. The TeraDrive itself launched in Japan on 31 May 1991.

At the time this particular machine looked to me like an early 1991 build, but its serial-number label had sadly been removed. Without that label I cannot substantiate its exact production date.
