Above is a demo of the TeraDrive running on VGA mode and composite video. Interestingly, the Genesis cartridges I tested did not play, while the PAL Mega Drive cartridges I tested did. That should not be taken as a blanket rule for all Genesis and PAL games, because Mega Drive compatibility varies by cartridge fit and by whether the individual game performs a region check. The compatible PAL games were running on the TeraDrive's 60Hz hardware, so timing differed from playing them on a normal 50Hz PAL Mega Drive. In Sonic's case the change was immediately noticeable in the music and overall pace. The way it was meant to be played. That is where this beast beats the Amstrad Mega PC. 60Hz is far more enjoyable than 50Hz.
