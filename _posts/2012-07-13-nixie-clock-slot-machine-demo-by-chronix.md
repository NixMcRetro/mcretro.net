Here's my prized Nixie clock from Chronix I got a few months back. Just thought I'd throw a video online showing it cycling through the cathode poisoning prevention routine (slot machine style!). Also, I should have some more photos and documentation up this weekend.

**Update 2013-04-23:** I replaced the NiCad battery in this particular clock with a NiMH cell and it worked successfully. I would no longer describe NiCd-to-NiMH replacement as a universally straightforward swap, however. Suitability depends on the clock's charging circuit, charge current and the particular replacement cell.
