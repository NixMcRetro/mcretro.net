You might remember the above video from a while back if you are a regular. The disc appears to spin backward and the laser mechanism goes berserk as well. Enter the BA6798S located on the left of the CD deck board. ROHM documents the BA6798S as a four-channel H-bridge BTL driver for CD-player motors and actuators. That makes it a plausible suspect when several parts of the drive mechanism are behaving abnormally, although these symptoms alone do not prove that the IC itself has failed. So once more the white Saturn gets put onto a shelf and sidelined while we wait for parts.
