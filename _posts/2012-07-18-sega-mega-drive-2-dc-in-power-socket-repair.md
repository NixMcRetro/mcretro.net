Got a bad feeling about your DC-in power socket on your Mega Drive 2? Disconnect the console from power before opening it and inspect where the DC-in socket connects to the mainboard. In this particular Mega Drive 2 the fault was a cracked solder joint, and resoldering the connection restored operation.
