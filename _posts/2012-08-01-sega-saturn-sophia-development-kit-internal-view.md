She's a bit dirty.

I'll give her the best clean I can and make her shine again.

The bigger problem is that this particular Sophia arrived without its SH-2 processor boards.

The Sophia development hardware uses removable SH-2 processor cards rather than soldering both Saturn CPUs directly onto the mainboard like a retail console.

Surviving development-hardware documentation shows that Programming Boxes could be configured with SH-2 cards and other development modules depending on what the developer needed. A two-SH-2 configuration provides the two processor paths expected by normal Saturn software.

Mine currently has neither processor card, so there is a limit to how far I can test it until I find suitable replacements.

If anyone has compatible Sophia SH-2 boards lying around, I'd be more than happy to take them off your hands and see how far she gets.
