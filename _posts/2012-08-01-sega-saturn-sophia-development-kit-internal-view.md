She's a bit dirty.

I'll give her the best clean I can and make her shine again.

The bigger problem is that this particular Sophia arrived without its SH-2 processor boards. The Sophia development hardware uses removable SH-2 processor cards rather than soldering both Saturn CPUs directly onto the mainboard like a retail console.

Sega's original Programming Box description lists one SH-2 card as supplied and says an additional SH-2 card is required when the box is used without an in-circuit emulator. Mine currently has neither processor card, so there is a limit to how far I can test it until I find suitable replacements.

If anyone has compatible Sophia SH-2 boards lying around, I'd be more than happy to take them off your hands and see how far she gets.

![](/assets/images/2012/img_0240.jpg)

I'll post much better photos once everything has been cleaned properly.

This eventually turned into a much larger restoration project. I later found compatible SH-2 cards and spent a good part of 2013 bringing the Sophia hardware back together.
