Today I just couldn't help myself. Dug into the VA0 PAL Dreamcast and took some photos of the internals. Interestingly it is definitely a VA0 board. There is no denying that. Later Dreamcast revision research has confirmed that PAL VA0 machines really do exist, although they are much less common than PAL VA1 consoles. This machine remains particularly interesting because its April 1999 case date and display-only label point to a pre-release history. I wonder what the difference is between it and say a Japanese VA0 or a US VA0 though. And how many PAL VA0 units are in the wild today? Not many at all!
