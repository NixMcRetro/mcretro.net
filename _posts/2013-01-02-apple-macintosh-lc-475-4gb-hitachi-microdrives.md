A friend loaned me some 4GB MicroDrives to try with the LC 475... It hasn't worked too well so far... If I had a USB reader of sorts I might have made progress. I'll have to track one down and retest. Something about a hidden partition.

Recent testing with a USB enclosure confirms there is a roughly 39MB partition at the beginning of these particular drives. That was probably left behind by whatever device originally used them rather than being an inherent feature of Hitachi's 4GB Microdrive itself. After repartitioning and formatting the drive with the HP USB Formatter Tool, we should be right to go. I have also found a Seagate MicroDrive to test with. We'll be back with part two in a few weeks.
