Here's the process of removing and installing the tantalum capacitors. They sure are a pretty good fit. I've used case size D instead of case size C. Either case size can be suitable where the electrical specifications and board footprint permit it. The larger package here is really a consequence of the capacitance, voltage rating and capacitor series I selected, rather than derating itself automatically requiring a larger case.

{% include youtube.html id="PaDyFWXIflo" %}

First power on with the new capacitors installed. There are 9x 47uF 25V (case size D) and 2x 100uF 16V (case size D) installed. Note the lack of fire! ;)


For more context on the derating rule I was using at the time, see [Apple Macintosh LC 475 Derating Tantalum Capacitors](/apple-macintosh-lc-475-derating-tantalum-capacitors/).
