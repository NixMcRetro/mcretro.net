Scanlines make the world go round.

This was my first proper test of the new VGA scanline generator I picked up through [ASSEMblergames](https://web.archive.org/web/20130109203054/http://www.assemblergames.com:80/forums/showthread.php?42388-Dreamcast-VGA-box-amp-Scanline-generator).

{% include youtube.html id="SY35O0Kryhs" %}

The more interesting part, though, was finally seeing the TeraDrive's Mega Drive mode running through a suitable display setup.

Sega designed the TeraDrive's analogue RGB output to handle both approximately 15 kHz and 31 kHz scan rates. The PC VGA side operates at the higher scan rate, while the Mega Drive video path uses the lower television-style scan rate. In other words, the 15 kHz Mega Drive output is a native feature of the TeraDrive. The scanline generator is a separate visual effect device rather than something that creates the TeraDrive's 15 kHz mode.

I had simply never bothered connecting my dual-frequency Amstrad CRT to the TeraDrive before.

Clearly I should have.

Mega Drive mode works beautifully.

Great news all around!
