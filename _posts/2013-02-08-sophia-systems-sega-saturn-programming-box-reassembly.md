Above is a brief video of the Sophia going back together. Well, halfway. The mainboard is back in, but the RAM board is still out because I need to photograph it properly first.

{% include youtube.html id="3E4Gvue9Im8" %}

And finally, after somewhere between six and eight months, I've finished photographing the internals of the Sophia Systems Sega Saturn Programming Box. Nearly 300 photos later, I can finally put the thing back together and reclaim the bench space for the Mega-CD development unit.

The big outstanding problem is still the processor hardware. My Sophia arrived without its removable SH-2 CPU boards. A normal dual-SH-2 configuration needs processor hardware in both positions, although development setups could also use specialised ICE or pod hardware instead of a second ordinary SH-2 board.

So, as we retire Sophia to the bookshelf for the moment, we embark on a new journey with the Sega Mega-CD.
