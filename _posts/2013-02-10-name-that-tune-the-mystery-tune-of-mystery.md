Here's a little musical mystery that I still haven't managed to solve. I'm thinking it might have come from an old keygen, perhaps some tracker music. Maybe a Commodore 64 game? Something from the Amiga? It's been so long now that I really haven't got a clue!

### Update: February 2013

Then I had another thought. What if this is old game music that sounds wrong because of the difference between PAL 50Hz and NTSC 60Hz hardware? Some older games tied their music playback to the refresh rate, and the developers didn't always account for the difference. So I made a faster version to see if that sounded any more familiar. Or possibly even wrong(er).
