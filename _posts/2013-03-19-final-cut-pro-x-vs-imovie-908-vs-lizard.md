I've decided my videos need background music. At the time I came across [Freeplay Music](https://freeplaymusic.com/) and thought I'd found the answer to my problems. No more relying on iMovie's built-in music!

As for the actual editing, Final Cut Pro X still doesn't quite *cut* it for me. I keep coming back to iMovie 9.0.8, even though it has an irritating collection of bugs. One of my favourites is the strange Cmd-H behaviour where iMovie disappears and doesn't seem particularly interested in returning. That's always helpful when you're trying to get a video finished.

For the quick videos I've been making, iMovie is still considerably easier for me to work with than Final Cut Pro X. It might have a few interesting ideas about how software ought to behave, but at least I know my way around it.

Of course, the real star of the video above isn't either editing application. It's the lizard that escaped from the 600 W power supply in the Core 2 Quad machine I rescued from the side of the road. Apparently the computer hardware around here has started arriving with its own wildlife.

**Update 8th February 2018:** Background music was a terrible idea...
