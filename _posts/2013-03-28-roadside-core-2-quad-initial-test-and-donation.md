As promised, here is the roadside Core 2 Quad machine in action during its initial testing. Considering it had spent the night outside in the rain, the useful hardware came through remarkably well. The contaminated power supply had already gone straight into the rubbish after the cockroach-and-lizard surprise, so this is the part of the story where we find out whether anything worth saving actually survived.

The first job was getting the motherboard into a condition where I could actually test it. The original Intel heatsink had come loose, thanks to those wonderful plastic retention clips. I swapped over a screw-mounted cooler from another system, which was a much more reassuring arrangement.

With a known working power supply connected, I gave the board its first test using the onboard VGA output. Initially there wasn't much happening, but eventually we had video. Excellent news! I'd already removed the rather rusty backup battery, so a few complaints about the configuration weren't exactly surprising.

Next up was the Radeon HD 5770 that had come with the machine. It needed an additional power connector, but with that attached the system started up and produced video again. That was a rather pleasant surprise considering where I'd found it.

The machine appeared to have a Core 2 Quad Q8400 running at around 2.66 GHz, along with 2 GB of RAM. It's a fairly respectable collection of hardware to find sitting on the side of the road. I still don't know why somebody threw the whole thing away, although the unpleasant surprises inside the case may have had something to do with it.

There were a few other things I'd need to sort out before handing it over, including a suitable case, storage and a power supply. Thankfully, I didn't need another PC for myself. I've already got more than enough machines scattered around the place.

Needless to say, the testing went well. The machine has now been donated to someone who is a complete Linux nerd. Happy compiling, Core 2 Quad! Not a bad second life for something that had been left on the side of the road.
