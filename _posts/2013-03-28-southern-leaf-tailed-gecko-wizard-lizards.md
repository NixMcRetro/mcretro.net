**2026 note:** The original version of this post mixed together information about different Australian leaf-tailed geckos. The Australian Museum uses "Southern Leaf-tailed Gecko" for *Phyllurus platurus*, while the Queensland Government also uses the common name for *Saltuarius swaini*. Because the original post did not record a scientific identification, I have removed the old claims of a ten-year wild lifespan and activity at -10°C. I could not substantiate those figures for the animal shown here.
