Here is a rip of a VHS tape given away with issue 3 (December 1992) of Mean Machines Sega. Apologies for the stuttering sound at times. I guess VHS tapes just weren't meant to last this long in perfect condition. The VHS tape box had this on it:

> Sega Mega-CD Coming to a Mega Drive near you Early 1993. Win an Incredible Sega Mega-CD! Details Inside. Sega Mega-CD Brings You... - 500 + Megabytes of real CD capacity - REAL music, - REAL voices, - REAL sound effects. - Can play audio CDs 68000 chips. - Zooming, scaling and rotation. Nothing else moves, looks, plays or sounds like it.

Games previewed on this tape include: Night Trap, Sewer Shark, Make your own music video, Batman Returns and Cobra Command.

**Coming soon to a Mega Drive near you in 1993.**
