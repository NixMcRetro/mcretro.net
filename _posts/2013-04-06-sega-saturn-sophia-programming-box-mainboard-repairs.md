After the A-Bus capacitor replacement failed to improve Sophia's condition, I moved on to the mainboard.

I've now replaced most of the electrolytic capacitors there, and the result is considerably more promising.

{% include youtube.html id="OBpd2jAA6E8" %}

![](/assets/images/2013/img_0388.jpg)

This is the first really strong indication that at least part of the Sophia's trouble is on the mainboard.

Replacing the majority of its capacitors produced a substantial improvement.

I still have several capacitors on backorder, so I am not calling the diagnosis completely finished yet.

![](/assets/images/2013/img_0386.jpg)

The CD mechanism still has its own known problems as well.

We already established that while testing the drive in a retail VA0 Saturn, so that remains a separate repair job.

I should probably inspect the power-supply boards too while I am this deep into it.

Apparently there is no such thing as replacing just one capacitor on Sophia.

The good news is that she is finally looking alive again.
