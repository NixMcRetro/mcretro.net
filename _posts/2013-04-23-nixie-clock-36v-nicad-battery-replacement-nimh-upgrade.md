A quick and easy repair/upgrade to my nixie clock. I hate NiCad batteries with a passion. Join me as I install a nice peaceful NiMH battery in place of it. Enjoy the back of my hand for half the video while I block what I am doing due to bad camera positioning! ;)

A NiMH battery is not automatically a drop-in replacement for NiCd in equipment that charges the battery internally. Suitability depends on the charging current and charge-control method because NiMH cells have different overcharge and trickle-charge requirements. This replacement worked in this clock, but anyone repeating the repair should check the charging circuit and the specifications of the replacement battery rather than relying on voltage alone.

**Update 2022-04-17**

![](/assets/images/2013/img_0389.jpg)

The battery was replaced late last year. IN-12B has a dot, IN-12A has no dot present. Some facts about the power supply, mostly for my own future reference.

- ID: 2.5 mm
- OD: 5.5 mm
- Length: 9.5 to 11 mm
- Input: 7.5 to 9 V DC
- Current: 300 mA or greater
- Polarity: centre positive

I've ordered an [XP Power ACM12US09](https://www.xppower.com/portals/0/pdfs/SF_ACM12.pdf) which should do the trick nicely. None of the Mean Well AC-to-DC power adapters are 2.5 mm, only 2.1 mm. They do offer an adapter but then I'd need to remember what the adapter... adapter is for.
