We can't blame this last one on the previous owner, a busted "super" cap. A glorified capacitor used to keep the clock alive while the Xbox is unplugged. There's nothing wrong with cell batteries, nor getting the time from the internet. Pre-1.6 Xboxes can run without the clock capacitor once it has been removed and any leakage cleaned up. Some 1.4 boards and all 1.6 systems used a different gold-coloured supercapacitor that is less prone to leaking, although 1.6 systems require a working clock capacitor or a suitable bypass to boot. The later Hynix-RAM variant is commonly referred to as 1.6b by the modding community.
