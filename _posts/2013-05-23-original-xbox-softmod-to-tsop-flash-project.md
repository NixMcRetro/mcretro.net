The terminology is worth keeping straight here.

A **softmod** changes what the Xbox can boot without replacing or rewriting its onboard firmware.

A **TSOP flash** is the later step where compatible original Xbox revisions have their onboard flash ROM made writable and reprogrammed with replacement firmware.

The machine I am working on is from the flashable pre-1.6 hardware family.
