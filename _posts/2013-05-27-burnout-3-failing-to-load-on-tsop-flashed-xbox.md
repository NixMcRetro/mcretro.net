For whatever reason, Burnout 3 hangs and refuses to load on the newly TSOP-flashed Xbox. I tried changing every relevant setting I could find in UnleashX with no success. Naturally, after all the work getting the Xbox repaired, softmodded, recovered and TSOP-flashed, I immediately suspected I had broken something else. Anyone got any ideas?

{% include youtube.html id="w7e3JzAubZ0" %}

Fortunately, the Xbox was innocent. A later check with help from HEX1GON at [ASSEMbler Games](https://web.archive.org/web/20191206184256/https://assemblergames.com/) traced this to a bad file from Underground Gamer.

So this was not a TSOP-flash compatibility problem and not an UnleashX configuration problem. It was simply bad game data. Sometimes the boring answer is the correct one.

In the meantime I still have Burnout Revenge, which is a personal favourite of mine.
