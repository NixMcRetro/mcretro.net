The power supply was arcing like crazy when power was connected. A little bit of a resolder/reflow with some fresh solder and it is as good as new.

One important caveat: this is a mains-voltage power supply, so it should be disconnected from AC before inspection and treated as potentially charged even after being unplugged. In this case, resoldering the cracked AC input joint stopped the arcing.
