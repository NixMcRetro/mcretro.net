Just some film from the cutting-room floor.

I thought it never hurts to see what is inside a SCART-style connector and how the wiring is actually arranged.

{% include youtube.html id="YHVazjlkvq0" %}

This is particularly relevant to the Framemeister because its supplied 21-pin RGB adapter uses the Japanese RGB21 arrangement rather than EuroSCART.

The connectors look extremely similar from the outside.

The pin assignments are not the same.

This video is really a physical comparison for future reference rather than another complete conversion guide.

I already documented the actual repinning work separately.

If nothing else, it is another reminder that matching connector shapes do not guarantee matching wiring.
