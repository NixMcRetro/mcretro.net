Just a quick peek at some Black Mesa madness! I've only recently come across it, and already I've found something that absolutely had to go on the internet. Any game with an *Office Space* reference earns instant respect points, especially when people start talking about TPS reports and coming in to work on the weekend.

Black Mesa is a free, fan-made reimagining of the original *Half-Life* using Valve's Source engine. The first version was released on 14th September 2012, so there's already plenty for me to play around with. I probably need a better GPU, though. That might make the experience a little more enjoyable!

Then I came across something even more interesting. Sitting in the game was a beige PC that looked remarkably similar to one I'd been working on over the past few days. The front had the same sort of arrangement, with three buttons, a badge, floppy drive bays, CD-ROM drive bays and even the vents in the case.

Mine is a little more yellow, of course! I've also been filling it with an assortment of hardware, including a Gravis Ultrasound, S3 video card, network card and Sound Blaster Live. It was quite a surprise to see such a familiar-looking computer sitting there in the game.

And because the game lets me interact with objects, I naturally decided to pick up the computer. That's mine now! I'm sure there will be more Black Mesa madness once I've spent a bit more time with it.
