Let us have a look at the Western Technologies Development Card functioning with a simple Sonic binary file. Uploaded (they call it downloading) from the PC to the Mega Drive. The comparison to a Mega EverDrive was actually fairly apt: the SegaDev card receives the binary from the PC into SRAM, while the Mega EverDrive could load ROMs from SD or send them from a PC over USB into its own cartridge memory. Beats having to burn the EPROMs with the game code every time you update code!
