More on the Western Technologies Sega Development Card and the software used to run it. `SEGALOAD.EXE` is the PC-side object-code downloader for the SegaDev hardware; it sends a compiled image from the development PC into the SRAM cartridge attached to the Mega Drive.

Here we look at some switches used and load up a little bit of Zero Wing. Ends up freezing, but that's only the start of problems... stay tuned! :)

The cause of the freeze was unresolved, so it did not establish that SEGALOAD itself was faulty. The later glitching video continues the investigation.
