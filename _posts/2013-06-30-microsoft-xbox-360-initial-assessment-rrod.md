Here's an Xbox 360 I picked up with the infamous Red Ring of Death.

At the time I was already ordering flux and repair gear and preparing for some sort of board-level repair.

The more important first diagnostic step should have been to record the console's secondary error code.

Three red lights tell us there is a serious hardware fault.

They do not, by themselves, identify the failed component.

The secondary code can narrow the fault to a much more useful area of the system.

I did not preserve that code in this post, so I cannot now give this particular console a more precise diagnosis without inventing one.

Cross your fingers and let's hope for the best!

Unfortunately, the later repair attempt was not successful.
