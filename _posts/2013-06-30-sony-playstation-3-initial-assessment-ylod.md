One Yellow Light of Death (YLOD) best served shaken, not stirred. A repair kit is on order, hopefully, it just needs a new pair of shims or a brand new hard drive. Time will tell. Sooner or later, time will tell.

That was very much the troubleshooting plan at the time. YLOD is a general hardware failure symptom rather than a diagnosis, so shims or a replacement hard drive should not be treated as general YLOD fixes. Later work using the PS3's SYSCON error logs made it possible to investigate the underlying fault much more directly.
