Here in the outback it sure can get hot, even in the middle of winter! My DG834Gv5 was running hotter than I liked, so I added some extra cooling.

The problem seemed to involve my Apple AirPort Express and AirPlay. With the router opened up and some heatsinks attached, everything behaved itself. Put the case back on, however, and the AirPort Express would mysteriously disappear from the network. Clearly something wasn't quite right!

My solution was to cut a hole in the top and install a 12 V fan I'd salvaged from another project. Running it at full speed was rather noisy, so I added a resistor to bring the voltage down to around 9 V. Much more civilised.

I also decided to have the fan blow fresh air into the case, directly over the heatsinks, rather than drawing air out. A bit of work with the drill, some sanding and a collection of mounting bolts later, and the fan was installed. Naturally, I hadn't measured everything quite as carefully as I probably should have.

The extra cooling sorted out the temperature problem I was seeing with this particular router. The fan runs whenever the modem is plugged in, which isn't much of an issue when the thing is normally running around the clock anyway.

Side note: I need a new modem!
