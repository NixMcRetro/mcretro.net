This is my attempt to repair the RROD Xbox 360 from the earlier assessment.

It did not work.

I hope you can at least learn from my mistakes.

The biggest diagnostic thing missing from this experiment is the console's secondary error code.

RROD is a broad hardware-failure indication, while the secondary code identifies a much narrower fault category.

I did not preserve that code here, so there is not enough evidence left in this post to say exactly what had failed.

That also means an unsuccessful heating, clamp or board-work attempt cannot tell us whether the underlying fault was something the attempted repair could ever have fixed.

For now, I'll stick with real retro consoles from the 16-bit era! :)
