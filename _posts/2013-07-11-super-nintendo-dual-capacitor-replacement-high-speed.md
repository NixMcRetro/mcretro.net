Decided to swap some caps over on all three of the SNES units. Here's two of them being done at the same time. 1CHIP is on the right. We eventually got them done, I ran out of "film" while recording because my camera needs a new, bigger SD card.

These old surface-mount electrolytic capacitors weren't looking particularly healthy, so we got stuck into removing them. Unfortunately, I managed to lift a solder pad at C65 on one of the older boards. The capacitor had been leaking around that area, which certainly wasn't helping matters.

After cleaning up the boards and preparing the solder pads, it was time to install the replacements. Some of the radial capacitors needed to sit quite low so they wouldn't interfere with the heatsink, particularly on the 1CHIP board. There was rather a lot of soldering involved!

The damaged pad also needed some special attention. Fortunately, I managed to make a connection through a nearby via and restore the circuit. Not the easiest little repair, but we got there in the end.

Sadly, replacing the capacitors didn't actually solve the original faults affecting the consoles. At least the work was completed, and the boards were looking considerably tidier. It also gave me another opportunity to practise my soldering, which is never a bad thing.
