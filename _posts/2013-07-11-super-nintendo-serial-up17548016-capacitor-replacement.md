We are recapping a 1CHIP SNES today and here's the process I follow and, as a general rule, follow whenever I do some recapping. I personally prefer radial electrolytics to SMD electrolytics. It doesn't look as pretty, but I find them easier to work with. Just look at Mega Drives with their million little caps, no leaks... yet! At the time I was very much in favour of proactive recapping once hardware reached this sort of age.

Capacitor life depends on the particular part and the conditions it has spent its life operating under, so radial does not automatically mean longer-lived than SMD, and age by itself does not mean every electrolytic capacitor needs replacing.
