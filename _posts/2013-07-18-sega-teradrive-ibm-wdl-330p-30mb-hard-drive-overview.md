Just some poking around inside one of the WDL-330P hard drives used with my TeraDrive.

Nothing terribly exciting if your definition of exciting excludes weird 30 MB hard drives.

The internet could certainly use more information about them.

{% include youtube.html id="pEyJgkUdXFo" %}

The WDL-330P uses the unusual IBM-family direct-bus storage arrangement I had already been investigating in the TeraDrive.

Its edge-style connector combines the drive interface and power in the same unusual cable arrangement.

It should **not** be confused with the superficially similar 44-pin laptop IDE connector.

I have personally confirmed WDL-330P drives working in the TeraDrive, which makes preserving these rather odd little disks particularly useful.

I eventually returned to the surviving drives in 2019 for a much more thorough recap and comparison.

For now, enjoy thirty megabytes of early-1990s storage glory.
