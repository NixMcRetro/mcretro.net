A quick look at an x0xb0x being built by my work colleague SuperNick (Nick).

The x0xb0x is a DIY analogue synthesiser project designed around recreating the sound and functionality of the Roland TB-303 bass synthesiser.

This particular build is based on the Adafruit-era x0xb0x hardware.

{% include youtube.html id="tLCHwp8Nhm0" %}

Since this video was made, SuperNick (Nick) has successfully tested parts of the board using an audio-card oscilloscope and confirmed both sawtooth and square-wave output.

That is a promising sign, although naturally it does not mean the entire synthesiser is finished and working yet.

If we're lucky he'll eventually make me some tunes for these videos.

I sometimes think I am going crazy from hearing the same six songs over and over and over and over.

Stay tuned! :D

The build did eventually reach the fully assembled stage.

Naturally, it then developed a no-sound problem.

Because apparently nothing around here is allowed to work immediately.
