Anyone get that *Oliver!* reference in the title of this video? Time for another look at the Aiwa Mega-CD CSD-GM1, and I've been going back through my notes to remind myself exactly where I left things. Thankfully I keep notes, or I'd have absolutely no idea what I'd previously done! :)

I've got two different mainboards to worry about, including the 01-01 and 02-01 revisions. The 02-01 board has been giving me quite a bit of trouble with cracking and damaged tracks, and there's also the question of where the 5 V supply to the Game Unit has disappeared to. I've already replaced a regulator during earlier work, but I still need to trace the power properly.

Neither of the units has been giving me the amplified audio I'd like, so there are a few more circuits to investigate. I've still got parts of the audio system and CD deck to work through, and I'm planning to check the voltage regulators and power lines before going much further.

Then something rather unexpected happened. While I was preparing to start measuring voltages, the CD player suddenly decided to behave! The display showed 13 tracks, and I had music playing without having done anything particularly clever. That was a nice surprise.

Unfortunately, all is not well. The unit is very sensitive to bumps and movement, and tilting it can cause some strange behaviour when the Game Unit is attached. I can see the old wavy video distortion again, and the Aiwa seems to be switching into a mode it shouldn't be in. There's clearly another connection or power problem hiding in there somewhere.

So that's where things stand. Some progress, some new questions, and another excuse to get the multimeter out. There's always something going wrong with these things, isn't there?
