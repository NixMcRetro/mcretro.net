Found that one of the Mic Amp Boards (Board 02-09) does not amplify sound. In this video, we look at why that might be and work to resolve the problem as best we can. The Mic Amp Board (01-09) from the revision 1 unit has no issues and was a good part to compare results with.

With the working and faulty boards sitting side by side, I started comparing the transistor readings and looking for differences between the two revisions. There were a few interesting component changes, including a capacitor that appeared to be installed backwards on the working board. Now that's confusing!

Unfortunately, swapping things about also revealed another problem with the Unit-02 mainboard, which wasn't producing sound properly. That board had suffered quite a bit of physical damage, so there was already plenty to investigate. Meanwhile, the microphone amplifier was still refusing to cooperate.

After a rather long session of poking around with the multimeter, I finally spotted two tiny breaks in the circuit traces. Two little jumper wires later and we had amplified audio again! Sometimes the smallest faults cause the biggest headaches.

Naturally, fixing that problem revealed another one. The Unit-02 volume board had very weak audio on its left channel, while the right channel was behaving itself. Still, both microphone amplifier boards were now working, and that's another little victory in the ongoing Aiwa Mega-CD adventure.
