Zip, zap and away! The idea here is simple: replace the soldered cartridge battery with a holder so that the next battery replacement does not require desoldering anything. SNES cartridges that use battery-backed SRAM rely on that battery to preserve their save data while the console is switched off.

Just solder in a holder and... well mine didn't have enough clearance inside the cartridge shell, but maybe yours does! **Check the height and shell clearance of the holder before soldering it in.** A perfectly good electrical solution is not terribly useful if the cartridge will no longer close.
