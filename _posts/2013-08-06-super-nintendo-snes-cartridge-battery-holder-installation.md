The idea here is simple: replace the soldered cartridge battery with a holder so that the next battery replacement does not require desoldering anything.

SNES cartridges that use battery-backed SRAM rely on that battery to preserve their save data while the console is switched off.

Naturally, my first battery holder had another idea.

It did not have enough clearance inside the cartridge shell.

So there is an important practical lesson hiding underneath the usual chaos:

**check the height and shell clearance of the holder before soldering it in.**

A perfectly good electrical solution is not terribly useful if the cartridge will no longer close.
