Just when you get one thing working, you discover a secondary fault on another board.

Unit-02 has an audio problem on the left channel.

Audio is leaking through, but the volume control for that channel is not behaving properly.

So this video is the diagnosis stage:

isolate the fault to the volume-control area, compare what the left and right channels are doing and work out where the behaviour diverges.

I later attempted to repair this board with contact cleaner and did not have much luck.

I eventually found another approach that worked better, but I do not have enough surviving documentation to reconstruct that later method confidently here.

For now, we at least know which board deserves the attention.
