Here's one method I was using at the time to clean stubborn game cartridge contacts. A pencil eraser certainly can remove grime from edge contacts, but I wouldn't now treat it as the default method for anything gold plated. Erasers are abrasive and can wear thin gold plating, so a gentler cleaning method appropriate to the particular connector should come first. And blowing on your carts is still a bad habit, Nintendo specifically warns against blowing on Game Pak contacts or getting them wet.
