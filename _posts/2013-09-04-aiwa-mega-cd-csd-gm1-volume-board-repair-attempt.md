Back to the faulty Aiwa volume board.

This repair attempt did not have much luck.

I tried using contact cleaner around the troublesome volume-control component, but in hindsight I used considerably more than I would today.

It appeared to remove or disturb grease that was supposed to remain in the mechanism.

Lucky for me, the component was already toast anyway!

I later found another way to deal with the board, but I do not have enough surviving notes to reconstruct that final method accurately.

So this video should remain what it actually is:

an unsuccessful repair attempt and a useful reminder not to drown mechanical controls in cleaner simply because the can is nearby.
