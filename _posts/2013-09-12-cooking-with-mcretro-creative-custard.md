Welcome to a late-night edition of Cooking with RetroJunkie! This is a new segment where I mix crazy and unheard-of ingredients to make a meal anyone can stomach. Well, except for those intolerant to delicious food. And lactose.

Tonight's culinary masterpiece involves a rather generous quantity of thick custard and some milk chocolate chips. I initially forgot to get a spoon, which isn't a fantastic start for a cooking show, but I remembered where they were eventually. Now we're getting somewhere!

The procedure is terribly complicated. Pour some chocolate chips into your container, add a load of custard, and sprinkle a few more chips on top to make it look fancy. Apparently it's also possible to add far too many chocolate chips. Who would have thought?

You're supposed to mix everything together, but my spoon isn't quite long enough to reach the bottom properly. That means I'll probably end up with a mountain of chocolate chips in the last few mouthfuls. Never mind, I've bought a whole litre of custard. I can always add more!

Why am I eating this ridiculous creation? I've been stuck back at work fixing things, and not even my own things. Other people's things! Long hours are apparently one of the great joys of being a technician, along with coming home and making yourself something that probably shouldn't qualify as dinner.

I've also got a random energy drink I picked up on special, just in case the custard and chocolate aren't enough. I suspect I may regret this combination, but that's a problem for later. Look at it! Absolutely wonderful. Mama's recipe, no doubt.
