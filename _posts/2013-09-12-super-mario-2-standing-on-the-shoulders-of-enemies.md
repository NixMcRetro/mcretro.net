Mario madness! Mario madness! I've got Super Mario Bros. 2 running on the Super Nintendo through Super Mario All-Stars, and I have one very simple question for Nintendo. Why change a formula that worked so well?

I'm much more of a Sega sympathiser than a Nintendo nutter, but I thought I'd give this another go. The problem is that I've spent most of my time trying to work out how to defeat the enemies. In the original Super Mario Bros., you jump on their heads and they're done for. Simple!

Here I jump on an enemy and end up standing on its head instead. What am I supposed to do with that? I thought I was playing Mario, not some strange game about using enemies as stepping stones. Perhaps I'm missing something obvious, but it's certainly making life difficult.

It would be like playing Kirby without being able to inhale anything. Imagine having to learn a completely different way to deal with the enemies! I suppose that's what happens when you spend too much time playing Sega games and then decide to give Nintendo another chance.

I figured it out in the end, but why change a formula that worked so well? Anyway, I'd better get back to spray-painting those old SNES cases. There's always another Nintendo-related problem waiting for me!
