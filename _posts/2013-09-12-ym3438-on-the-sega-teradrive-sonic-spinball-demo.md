A little Sonic Spinball oddity on the Sega TeraDrive.

The music keeps playing after entering the options area.

The TeraDrive is particularly interesting here because it uses a discrete Yamaha YM3438 for FM audio.

The YM3438 is closely related to the YM2612 used in ordinary early Mega Drive hardware, but this should not be reduced to:

"put a YM3438 in a Model 1 and get TeraDrive sound."

The YM3438 has different analogue output characteristics, and the circuitry around the chip affects the final result.

So the observation in this video is specifically:

**this is how Sonic Spinball behaves on my TeraDrive's native YM3438 audio hardware.**

A modified Mega Drive using a substituted chip is not necessarily an electrically identical comparison.
