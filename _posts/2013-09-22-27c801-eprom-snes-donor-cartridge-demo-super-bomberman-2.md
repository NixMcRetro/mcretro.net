This was a quick video to support the previous video on AM29F032 TSOP40 flashing but got cut as I forgot about it! We run Super Bomberman 2 using a 27C801 EPROM on the NHL 96 or perhaps World Cup Striker donor cart. They look the same so I can't tell the difference!

The 27C801 is an 8 Mbit (1 MiB) EPROM. Windowed versions can be erased with UV light; one-time-programmable versions cannot.
