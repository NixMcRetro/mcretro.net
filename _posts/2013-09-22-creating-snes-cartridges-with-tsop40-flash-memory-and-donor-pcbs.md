Here we create a SNES cart using a donor cart - either NHL 96 or World Cup Striker. We will be flashing two games, EarthBound and Donkey Kong Country 3.

The conversion reuses the donor PCB and supporting circuitry, replacing the original mask ROM with programmable TSOP40 flash memory. A suitable donor and the correct adapter wiring still matter; these game names alone are not a compatibility checklist for every cartridge.
