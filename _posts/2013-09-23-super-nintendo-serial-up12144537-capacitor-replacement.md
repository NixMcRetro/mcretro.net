Replacing the electrolytic capacitors in Super Nintendo serial **UP12144537** with surface-mount canned replacements.

No long radial leads here.

That should make some of the purists happy!

I accidentally ordered some of the replacements in a smaller case size than the originals.

They fit nicely, but physical size alone does not establish electrical equivalence.

The important checks remain the actual capacitor specifications:

- capacitance;
- voltage rating;
- polarity;
- temperature rating;
- and, where the circuit makes it relevant, ESR and ripple-current capability.

A modern component may genuinely be physically smaller than an older equivalent, but "it fits" is not the specification.

Overall this one came together rather nicely. :)
