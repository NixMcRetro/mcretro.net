Replacing the electrolytic capacitors in Super Nintendo serial **UP12144537** with their canned brethren. No long legs here, that should make some of the purists happy!

Overall it looks pretty good and was quite easy to do after I picked the wrong case size when ordering (one size smaller). It's amazing how small replacement components can be! :) A smaller case alone does not establish electrical equivalence; capacitance, voltage rating, polarity, temperature rating and, where relevant, ESR and ripple-current capability still matter.
