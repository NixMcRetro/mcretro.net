Some footage of me playing around with Sega Mega Drive 2 serial **440005313** a while back. Some interesting results. I suspect that the 315-5660 chip has some issues. Let's try replacing some of the other chips first in the next couple of videos. Also, stay tuned for a very special music video that was inspired by this footage.

The follow-up posts on 28 September record VRAM, video-encoder and AV-socket replacements that did not eliminate the distortion. Those negative results leave the 315-5660 as a suspicion rather than a confirmed diagnosis.
