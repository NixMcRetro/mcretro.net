Some footage of me playing around with a Sega Mega Drive 2 that has some rather spectacular video distortion.

**Console serial: 440005313**

My main suspicion at this stage was the Sega 315-5660 ASIC.

Before condemning the large custom chip, though, I decided to try some of the more accessible possibilities first.

The following repair experiments included:

- replacing the VRAM;
- replacing the video encoder;
- replacing the AV socket.

None of those repairs eliminated the distortion.

That makes them useful negative results, but it does **not** prove by itself that the 315-5660 was definitely faulty.

I do not appear to have documented a final confirmed repair for serial `440005313`, so the ASIC remains a historical suspicion rather than a proven diagnosis.
