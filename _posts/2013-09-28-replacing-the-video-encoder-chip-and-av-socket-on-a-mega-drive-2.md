Another repair experiment on Mega Drive 2 serial **440005313**.

This time I replaced the video encoder and the AV socket to see whether either was responsible for the distortion.

They were not.

Or, more precisely:

replacing them did not make the symptom go away.

That distinction matters because an unsuccessful replacement is a useful troubleshooting result, but it is not absolute proof that every part of that signal path is perfect.

The VRAM replacement also failed to solve the problem.

So the list of easy suspects is getting shorter while the large 315-5660 ASIC continues sitting there looking increasingly suspicious.

Rather than bore you with 45 minutes of footage that does not achieve the goal I had hoped for, enjoy the shortened version! ;)
