Here's a quick overview of PSIO prototype hardware revision 1.1.

At this point PSIO was still very much development hardware.

The project was working towards loading PlayStation software without relying on the console's optical drive, using the PlayStation's parallel I/O interface and external storage hardware.

Revision 2.0 was already in the works while I was testing this 1.1 unit.

Over the next few weeks I'll be putting this particular prototype through a selection of games and documenting what works, what partly works and what completely falls over.

Those compatibility results belong specifically to the prototype hardware and software I was testing.

They should not be read as compatibility claims for later PSIO revisions or the eventual retail product.

Stay tuned for some interesting times! ;)
