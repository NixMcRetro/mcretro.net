Here's the Lunchbeat actually doing its thing.

The Lunchbeat is a small DIY groovebox designed by Buranelectrix around an Atmel ATmega328P.

The sounds themselves are generated as 1-bit signals and combined through a simple resistor-based output stage.

More importantly:

it makes noises.

Which is why this is the demo rather than the introduction.

I was hoping to build another one on a properly fabricated PCB later, so naturally this little project was not finished tormenting me yet.
