Here's the Lunchbeat, a DIY 1-bit groovebox created by Buranelectrix.

This version is built around an Atmel ATmega328P running at 16 MHz.

The original design combines six buttons, five potentiometers, eight LEDs and a simple resistor DAC into a wonderfully compact little noise machine.

The sound generation is intentionally primitive:

individual sounds are 1-bit signals that are mixed together before reaching the output stage.

That is considerably more interesting than it has any right to be.

I'll be making another one on a properly fabricated PCB in the future.

For now, the next post actually lets this one make some noise.
