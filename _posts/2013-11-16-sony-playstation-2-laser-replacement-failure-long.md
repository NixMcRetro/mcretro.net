Damn, I just don't plan well enough when it comes to these modern-day consoles. PS2, you upset me so. If you don't want to watch 24 minutes of failure, hold tight for the short and sweet version coming up next!

I've got a PlayStation 2 here that will happily play audio CDs, but refuses to recognise PlayStation 1 games. Unfortunately, I don't have any PlayStation 2 games or DVD movies handy to test the rest of the drive properly. Not a particularly comprehensive collection of test media, is it?

Fortunately, there's another PS2 nearby that powers on but produces no picture or sound. Perhaps I can borrow its optical drive and get somewhere with this one. That was the plan, anyway!

After spending a rather ridiculous amount of time dismantling the consoles, I discovered that the optical assemblies weren't quite as interchangeable as I'd hoped. They looked similar at first glance, but the differences became rather more obvious once everything was out on the bench.

The laser assemblies themselves also turned out to be incompatible. Wonderful! Since the donor machine was already destined for spare parts, I continued pulling its laser mechanism apart, although things didn't exactly improve from there. By the end I was more or less demonstrating how not to remove a delicate optical pickup.

So there you go. No successful laser replacement, no miraculous PlayStation recovery, just a considerable amount of dismantling and another reminder to check component compatibility before getting too enthusiastic with the screwdriver!
