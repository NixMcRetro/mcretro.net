With the chunky PlayStation 2 attempts out of the way, I moved on to the slim machines.

This video shows the laser-assembly swap on the particular PS2 Slim I was working with.

It should not be treated as a universal pickup guide for every slim revision.

Different PS2 Slim models use different optical pickups, so identify the original part before ordering a replacement.

Replacement pickups may also arrive with an anti-static solder bridge that must be removed before use.
