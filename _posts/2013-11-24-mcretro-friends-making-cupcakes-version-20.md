CUPCAKES! They worked! Never believe the hype! Makes 24 small or 12 large! :)

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup caster sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup (125 mL) milk, maybe 3/4 cup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 125 g butter
- pinch of salt

**Method**

Put everything in a bowl, then mix and put in cupcake patties. Then cook. Then eat. Victory will be yours, my friend!
