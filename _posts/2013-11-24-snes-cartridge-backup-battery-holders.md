I have had an absurd amount of fun trying to find a sensible SNES cartridge battery holder.

My earlier holder experiments had one major problem:

**clearance.**

A battery holder can work perfectly on the bench and still be useless if the cartridge shell will not close around it.

At this point I was seriously considering giving up on the holder idea and simply using properly tabbed replacement coin cells instead.

Sometimes the elegant reusable solution is not actually the most practical solution for the available space.

The important thing is to use a suitable replacement for the cartridge and install it with the correct polarity.

This saga has gone on considerably longer than a coin-cell holder deserves. :)
