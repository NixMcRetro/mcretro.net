Look who I picked up on my way home today. Two sassy ladies, I like to call Sophia 2 and Sophia 3. Now there is even more to do! More on their repairs as we go! They are Sega Saturn dev kits and originated from the UK.

Unfortunately, neither of these Sega Saturn programming boxes is particularly happy. The first one has no video output, which is rather familiar territory after my adventures with the original Sophia. There's also some cosmetic damage, including scratches and a rather unhappy-looking power switch.

One nice difference is that these UK units run on 240 V, so there's no need for the enormous transformer I've been using with the Japanese machine. There are also some interesting differences in the cases and circuit boards, despite the hardware being broadly similar.

The second unit isn't producing video either, and it's missing one of its little front-panel buttons. I'll start by investigating the capacitors, since that worked rather well last time. With three Sophia units available, I can also swap some of the boards around to help narrow down the faults.
