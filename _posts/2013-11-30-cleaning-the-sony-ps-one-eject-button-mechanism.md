Mary Poppins would be proud, shining up the old PS one eject-button mechanism. No idea what Mary Poppins is? Shame on you! (Or maybe your parents!)

Another PlayStation with an eject button that doesn't want to cooperate. You can give it a little wiggle and it'll eventually behave, but that's hardly a particularly satisfactory way to open a console. Let's see what's hiding underneath.

Once the button was removed, the problem became rather obvious. There was a considerable amount of grime around the plastic sliding surfaces, including something that looked suspiciously like chocolate syrup. Wonderful! No wonder the thing was getting stuck.

After pulling the mechanism apart, I gave the button and its surrounding plastic components a thorough clean. There was plenty of accumulated rubbish to remove, but fortunately nothing appeared to require replacing.

The improvement was immediate. The button was moving much more smoothly, and after reassembly it was behaving properly again. I'd considered adding some ceramic grease, but cleaning alone had made such a difference that it wasn't necessary for this particular repair.

I also had a quick look at the power button, which wasn't quite as troublesome but certainly wasn't spotless either. It's amazing how much grime can accumulate inside these little mechanisms over the years.
