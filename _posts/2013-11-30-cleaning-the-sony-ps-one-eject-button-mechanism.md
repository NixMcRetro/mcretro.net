Mary Poppins would be proud, shining up the old PS one eject-button mechanism. No idea what Mary Poppins is? Shame on you! (Or maybe your parents!)

Dirt and friction can make the plastic mechanism stick, although damaged parts can cause similar symptoms. Clean the moving surfaces carefully and check that they move freely before reassembly. If lubrication is needed, use only a very small amount known to be compatible with the plastic. No need to drown the thing.
