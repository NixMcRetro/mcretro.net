After I nuked my SNES EarthBound save, I realised that backing up saves might be a good idea.

I also discovered that being tethered to the television for half a week while playing EarthBound day and night was not necessarily ideal.

So I finished it on my Nintendo DS Lite instead.

I did not record the emulator name in this 2013 post.

Years later, in 2022, I found the old save files again and was able to identify the setup as **SNEmulDS**.

Those surviving saves dated back to around October 2013 and included Ness and company at roughly levels 75 and 97.

So at least some of the save-game history I thought I had lost eventually resurfaced.

The emulator was not perfect.

The music could be glitchy.

But it was good enough for me to complete the entire game on a handheld.

Here is the ending to EarthBound, completed at perhaps 3 or 4 am on a weekday.
