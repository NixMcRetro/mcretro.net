After I nuked my SNES save game, I realised I wanted to back up my save games! Emulation does come in handy once in a while, and I couldn't be tethered to the TV screen for half a week while I played day in, day out. So I finished EarthBound on my Nintendo DS Lite instead.

Anyway, here is the ending to EarthBound, completed at 3 or maybe 4 am on a weekday. Wow! So scare! Much spoiler! Music is a bit glitchy, of course; the emulator isn't 100%, but it was good enough to complete the entire game! :D

I didn't record the emulator name in this 2013 post. In 2022 I found old EarthBound save files that appeared to date back to October 2013, with saves at levels 75 and 97. It looked like I'd been using SNEmulDS.
