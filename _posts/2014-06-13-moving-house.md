And this is why my videos have been so spotty since December. I've had to move house, and it has been a pain! Problems with the neighbours were getting a bit ridiculous, so I decided to get away from the whole situation before it drove me mad. Unfortunately, moving a retro collection isn't quite as simple as throwing a few clothes in a suitcase!

I'm still between homes, with most of my cool things packed away in boxes. There's hardware everywhere, and I'm slowly working out where everything is supposed to go. Even I don't know what's in some of the boxes until I open them!

I've got the Pioneer LaserActive and its Mega Drive PAC here, along with various Saturns, Mega Drives, Mega CDs, Game Gear games and a Multi-Mega. There's even some PSIO hardware, revision 1.1, somewhere in the collection. Finding anything I actually want to work on is becoming an adventure in itself.

I've also found some rather handy drawers for storing controllers and AV cables. Fantastic idea, until I realised I'd filled them all and had nowhere left to put my clothes! So now the clothes are in bags. Priorities, people!

The little blue shelves are rather good for stacking the Mega Drive and Mega CD, although I've still got plenty of equipment to unpack. Some of the Saturns haven't even made it here yet, and I can't remember which box contains the older PlayStations. At least I keep finding things I'd forgotten about, including a Nixie clock without its power adapter and a pile of LaserDiscs.

It's also hotter here, and the cockroaches are enormous. Practically the size of a slipper! I've put out some cockroach baits in the hope of keeping them away from everything. The last thing I need is the local wildlife deciding to move into the collection too.

I have managed a few repairs, though, and those videos should be appearing over the next few weeks. There'll still be the occasional random video whenever I find something worth showing, but getting properly settled and back into regular repairs is going to take a while.

For now, it's boxes, boxes and more boxes. Slowly, day by day, I'm getting there. Thanks again for watching, and we'll see you in the next video. McRetro out!
