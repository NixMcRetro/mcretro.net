We delve a little deeper into the Mattel-version NES and have a look at the cartridge connector.

At the time I could find replacement connectors for about $10 shipped on eBay. The replacement I had in hand did not feel as robust as the original, and its outer metal shield came off much more readily.

Treat that as an observation about the replacement part I actually handled, not every aftermarket NES connector ever made.

And if you somehow make the cartridge slot catch fire, you've probably gone well beyond ordinary cleaning. :P
