In this video I repair one of the Sega Saturn Sophia development systems first assessed back in November 2013.

**Model:** SG4101  
**Serial:** H417010070

This unit powered up without usable video. On this particular machine, replacing the failed electrolytic capacitors restored the video output.

If you were muttering "I bet it's capacitors" at the screen, you were right this time!

That is the result of this repair, not a claim that every no-video Sophia fault is caused by capacitors.
