Newly hatched Willie Wagtail chicks here in NSW, Australia! The adults loved to swoop us like the magpies do and made sharp clicking and scolding noises whenever we got too close. Steer clear!

Hopefully the babies grow up fast and go away so we can go outside once more. ;)

Willie Wagtails (*Rhipidura leucophrys*) actively defend their territory, which helps explain the swooping around the nest.

**Update December 2014:** They have left the nest empty and no longer swoop, but make lovely calls, much like Australian Magpies! Much more civilised!
