This poor iMac was left in the garage. It already had a bad analogue board, but now it has another problem. Bugs... or rather, an entire ant nest inside the machine! There are hundreds of the little buggers running around in there!

They've even been laying eggs inside the case, with ants scurrying up and down the front bezel trying to rescue them. I can't imagine they're terribly happy about having their home disturbed. Where's the queen? Somewhere in that mess, presumably!

I've pulled out the optical drive for a closer look, and guess what? More ants! There's also a disc inside that looks rather familiar. It might actually be one of mine, so perhaps I'll retrieve it and see what's on there.

Don't leave your iMacs sitting in the garage. That's the lesson here! Thankfully, I managed to _debug_ this one by letting the ants gather their eggs and move out before sending the poor machine off for e-waste recycling.

The disc did turn out to be mine and had Munchies and Maniac on it! Both great games for the Mac platform!
