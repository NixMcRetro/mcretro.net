Geez! I sure have been absent as of late. I guess my non-internet life gets in the way at times! I'll do my best to bring this channel up to speed in the next few months. I've definitely got more videos in the pipeline, so sit down, kick your feet up and enjoy! :D

First up, be sure to visit McRetro.net. RetroJunkie.net has gone off to live with somebody else, apparently in Japan, so I've moved everything under the one McRetro umbrella. Makes things a bit easier, doesn't it?

The new site is running on a Raspberry Pi 2. It's a wonderful little machine, although the site is serving pages at around 3.6 kB/s at the moment. That's roughly 28.8k dial-up territory! Takes me right back to the first internet connection I remember using in the mid-1990s. Perhaps I can share that authentic experience with everybody!

I've also got a stack of videos to upload, including the Lunchbeat and PICkit 2 adventures. Some of those recordings have been sitting around since January, or even longer. I'll finally get them out there, along with some more Sega Mega Drive 2 tinkering.

And did you notice the new intro? It's the same as the old one, except now there's smoke! Which do you prefer, smoke or no smoke? Leave a comment and let me know. It's good to be back!
