With Google preparing to retire Picasa, I decided to try some alternatives.

In 2016 Flickr seemed like the best fit for the photo gallery, and for the sake of scattering my pictures absolutely everywhere I also put them on Facebook.

I left the old Picasa material up for as long as it remained available and wondered whether everything would eventually disappear into Google+ too. 🙃

Flickr did not remain the permanent answer either.

That account was eventually closed, so the current McRetro photo archive now lives in the site's own [Photo Gallery](/photos).
