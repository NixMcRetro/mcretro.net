With [Google preparing to retire Picasa](https://googlephotos.blogspot.com/2016/02/moving-on-from-picasa.html), I decided to try some alternatives. Flickr seemed like the best fit for the photo gallery, and for the sake of scattering my pictures absolutely everywhere I also put them on Facebook.

I left the old Picasa material up for as long as it remained available and wondered whether everything would eventually disappear into Google+ too. 🙃

**Update, 7 May 2022:** The Flickr account was closed, and I planned to move the photo archive to the site's own [Photo Gallery](/photos). That destination currently shows a placeholder; the gallery has not been restored there.
