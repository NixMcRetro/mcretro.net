There's nothing I enjoy more than finding a computer on the side of the road. So what happens when I find **two** computers on the side of the road? Naturally, I hook both of them up to see whether they work. Turns out they mostly do! If I lived near a rubbish tip, my house would probably be full of computer parts.

The first machine is a 3.2 GHz Pentium 4 with 3 GB of RAM and a rather impressive rainbow fan. Somebody has somehow managed to break one of its USB ports clean off. How do you even manage that? Neither computer has a hard drive, which is a shame because I don't have any spare older drives handy either.

The second one is a Duron 650 with about 393 MB of RAM and bulging capacitors all over the motherboard. It wasn't particularly pleased about being switched on, although it did still run. Both machines are going to need some attention, but that's not bad for a rubbish collection!

The timing was also useful because I was looking for an AT or ATX power supply for the Sega TeraDrive experiments. Apparently I needed 5 A on the 12 V rail. Or was it the 5 V rail... Yeah, clearly I hadn't confirmed that part yet. :P So don't take that as a TeraDrive power specification. That's another video for the near future.
