Today we have a look around the outside of the Sega TeraDrive Model 2.

The TeraDrive combines an IBM PC-compatible 80286 computer with Mega Drive hardware in the same chassis. Sega sold three models in Japan with different RAM and storage configurations.

The Model 2 has 1 MB of PC-side RAM as standard and two floppy drives.

There are also some obvious design similarities to IBM machines of the period, which is what caught my attention in the original video.
