Ever sit back and wish your room was only 27 degrees?

This particular Dimplex DC10RC was doing an extremely poor job of getting me there.

In my room I was seeing something like a two-degree reduction compared with the outside temperature, despite using it in a room smaller than the space I expected it to handle.

I originally pointed to the manual's **43°C** figure as if that meant the unit should still deliver a particular temperature drop at 43°C.

That's not what the specification says.

The manual says the unit can be operated in cooling mode with ambient temperatures between **17°C and 43°C**. That is an operating range, not a guarantee of how many degrees it will pull a particular room down.

Something still seemed wrong with the performance of this particular unit, and the later DC15RCBW behaved dramatically better for me.
