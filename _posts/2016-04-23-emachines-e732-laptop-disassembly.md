As I was pulling apart an eMachines E732 for a friend, I realised I should make a video for anybody else unfortunate enough to be doing the same thing.

These machines are reasonably straightforward to disassemble, with accessible RAM, hard drive and wireless card.

I originally said the CPU might be replaceable "if it is socketed" because I had not checked.

The service guide answers that one.

The E732 family uses an rPGA processor package and the official service procedure includes removing and replacing the CPU, so the processor is indeed socketed on the documented models.

Some E732-family variants also shipped with additional graphics hardware, which explains the extra cooling and internal differences I vaguely remembered seeing.
