But did it ever leave? Enjoy just over ten minutes of rambling by your favourite host, Nix McRetro! Don't worry, the hair isn't a permanent solution. Although it is artsy, isn't it? Am I that hip? Sir, no sir!

A few things have been happening since the last proper update. For one, I've managed to lose about 36 kg since January, which is quite an extraordinary change over seven or eight months. I'd started somewhere around 115 kg and was down to approximately 79.4 kg when I recorded this video. I'm rather pleased with the progress, although it still feels a little strange seeing how much things have changed.

I spent some time talking about the food and activity changes I'd made, along with the weight and body-composition measurements I was keeping. I've got a set of Withings scales that provide various estimates, although I'm well aware those body-fat figures aren't perfectly accurate. They're more useful for following a general trend than treating every number as gospel.

I was also wondering where I wanted things to settle down, rather than continuing with the same rate of change indefinitely. This was very much a personal update about what I'd been doing, not some universal diet plan. Everybody's circumstances are different, and the numbers from one person's experiment don't necessarily tell anybody else what they should do.

Unfortunately, the same sense of achievement hadn't quite carried over into my studies. I'd signed up for an online programming course and was becoming increasingly frustrated with the way the lessons were written. The first week made reasonable sense, and then the material seemed to leap ahead without actually explaining some of the things I needed to know. Wonderful!

I was thinking about whether to continue with that course, particularly with my plans to study biomedical science in 2017. It's not that I dislike programming, but I do enjoy science, and there's only so much time available for everything. Besides, there's an entire room full of retro hardware waiting to be repaired.

Speaking of which, there are quite a few projects still underway. I've been working on some Sega Mega Drive 2 VRAM experiments, including the idea of crossing address lines for interesting graphical results. Then there's the Sega TeraDrive, which seems determined to remain an ongoing project, and the Dreamcast development kit sitting somewhere behind me.

There's also a little McRetro shop and some equipment I've been trying to sell. I'm perfectly happy helping people with their machines, but running a shop isn't something I've always dreamed of doing. I'd rather spend the time tinkering with electronics than packing boxes all day.

So there you go. A new hairstyle, a fairly substantial personal update, some frustration with study and an impressive collection of unfinished hardware projects. Pretty much everything you'd expect from ten minutes of me talking to a camera.

I've missed you guys. More videos coming, more repairs to attempt and quite possibly more mistakes to document. I really do need a haircut though!
