Today's job is replacing C59 on a PAL SNSP-CPU-01 Super Nintendo mainboard. Everything else has already been recapped, so there's just one lonely capacitor left to deal with. I've got a 10 uF, 25 V bipolar replacement ready to go.

C59 is a rather interesting position because Nintendo didn't keep it consistent across every board revision. The -01 revision and some -02 boards were fitted with a polarised capacitor at C59, while most -02 boards use a bipolar or non-polar capacitor. Looks like somebody had a change of heart somewhere along the way!

The two boards are extremely similar, although the CPU-02 has a few small layout changes, including an additional C67 mounting option. It's worth checking exactly what's fitted before replacing anything.

The Hakko tweezers came in handy for this one. Incredible how well they worked! Leaked SMD capacitors are mostly what I deal with, unfortunately, so I'll happily take a repair that doesn't turn into an enormous headache.

With C59 replaced, it's time to power the console up and see whether it still works. Desert Fighter will do nicely for a test! Everything appears to be running as it should, so that's one more capacitor out of the way.
