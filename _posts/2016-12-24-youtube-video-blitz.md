In the next week I'll be editing a whole bunch of footage, in excess of 250 GB! It's going to be quite a task, especially since Apple has changed the interface on Final Cut Pro X (now up to 10.3.1), making me have to hunt down basic settings. At least the keyboard shortcuts still work well.

I've allocated an entire week on my calendar to processing YouTube videos, so hopefully I'll get through quite a few of them. I'm hoping to have access to a slightly faster machine too, because encoding 1080p footage of yesteryear's hardware takes a long time. Once they're ready, I'm planning to upload them from the university. My home ADSL2 connection uploads at around 80 KB/s, and it seems determined to choke the rest of the internet while it's doing it!

There's also a rather hefty Sega Dreamcast development kit making an appearance in the Christmas video. An HKT-0120, I think. I very nearly drop it while talking about my weight loss, and Siri has absolutely no idea how to convert kilograms into cakes. Surely it depends on the size of the cake! :-)

And a Merry Christmas to everyone while I'm at it! :)
