Here's some old aquarium footage from SuperNick (Nick), back when he was keeping shrimp and Corydoras catfish. These little fellows were quite fond of hiding amongst the greenery, although there's one thing that could tempt them out. Food!

For such miniature catfish, they certainly know how to make a meal into a major event. Look at them go!

One of these Corydoras survived into the later BioPod days, and from memory we purchased some more around 2019 or 2020. I'm not entirely sure whether any of them actually appeared in the later BioPod recordings, which mostly featured guppies and Endler's livebearers.

Quite a few years of fishkeeping history between these little recordings!
