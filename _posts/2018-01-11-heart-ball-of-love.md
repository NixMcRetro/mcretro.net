Just when we thought the rattle ball wars couldn't get any worse, the heart-shaped rattle ball has come under attack! Apparently a computer virus from the 23rd century has travelled all the way back to target this innocent little toy. How did it end up in the 21st century? We simply do not know!

Tillie and Pudge have already been busy with their other rattle ball adventures, so perhaps it was only a matter of time before the heart ball was dragged into the conflict. Somebody call the computer repairer. Or perhaps just give the birds another toy!
