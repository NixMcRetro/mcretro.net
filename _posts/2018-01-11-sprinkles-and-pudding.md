Meet Sprinkles and Pudding! Or should that be Tillie and Pudge? These two little green-cheeked conures have apparently acquired some additional names, because one name per bird simply isn't enough around here.

Tillie, otherwise known as Sprinkles, and Pudge, otherwise known as Pudding, are compact little feathered troublemakers belonging to the *Pyrrhura* genus. They enjoy hanging about, sometimes upside down, and generally making themselves the centre of attention.

Perhaps one day we'll have a third upside-down bird to join the family. Until then, there's plenty of mischief to be had with these two. Stay tuned for more adventures! :)
