It's feast time for Sprinkles and Pudding! Or Tillie and Pudge, if we're using their more familiar names. The two little green-cheeked conures have been settling into their new home throughout January, and apparently a good meal is another excellent excuse for a bird adventure.

With toys to attack, places to climb and snacks to investigate, the pair certainly haven't been short of things to do. Today it's the food taking centre stage. Enjoy your feast, you two little turkeys!
