A freshly cleaned cage, a lovely supply of drinking water and two conures who simply can't get enough of it. Tillie and Pudge have had quite a busy first month at home, so perhaps a good drink is exactly what's needed after all that running around.

Apparently the water is so clean that there's no reason to stop. Cheers, birdies! Just remember that the drinking bowl is meant for drinking, although we've already seen how Tillie feels about that particular rule.
