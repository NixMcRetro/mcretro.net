Baby Borb Chip has only been home for a little over a week, and feeding time is still quite the production! Our little green-cheeked conure is enjoying some nice warm bird milk, although getting all of it into the tummy is apparently optional.

Chip proceeds to get food everywhere before making a break for freedom. Never mind the mess, there are important Baby Borb things to do, and sticking around for the clean-up clearly isn't one of them!

It's been a busy few days for Chippy, with plenty of feeding, sleeping and growing to do. There's even a first bath documented on the same date. What a busy little borb!
