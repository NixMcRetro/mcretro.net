I've never had an air conditioner spit ice at me before. It was a little bit strange to be sitting below it typing away on my computer when suddenly a chunk of ice flies out and hits me in the back of the head! :P

Inside, a thin sheet of ice had built up around the cooling fins and thermostat. There's even an "antivirus" filter under there, which gave me a bit of a laugh! Switching to fan mode quietened things down, but it's not exactly what an air conditioner is meant to be doing.

It's generally been a pretty good unit, so I'll see how things go after cleaning the air filters. Perhaps that drainage pipe needs attention too. Samsung, you've done it again!
