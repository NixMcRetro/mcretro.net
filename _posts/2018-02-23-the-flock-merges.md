It's time for Chip, our newest arrival, to become better acquainted with Pudge, The Boss! After arriving earlier this month and spending time separately from the older birds, our little Baby Borb is now getting a closer introduction. They're meeting outside the cage, while Tillie, the second-in-command, waits inside to help keep the peace.

What could possibly go wrong? The meeting comes with a bit of a surprise for Tillie and Pudge, and there's certainly plenty for everyone to get used to. It's another step towards bringing the whole flock together, although Chip still has some settling in to do. Welcome to the ongoing adventure, Chippy!
