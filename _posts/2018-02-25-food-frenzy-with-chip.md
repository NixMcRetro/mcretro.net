Look at little Chippy go! It wasn't long ago that Baby Borb was making a tremendous mess of those warm feeds, but now Chip is growing steadily and getting used to the wonderful world of solid food.

Corn, apple, sultanas and bird seed are all on the menu, and there's certainly plenty for a growing little conure to investigate. Just about everything is still new and exciting.

Chip has also been gradually getting acquainted with Tillie and Pudge, although the newest arrival is still spending much of the time separately from the older two birds. One adventure at a time, Chippy! First we'll master the food, then we'll work on taking over the household.
