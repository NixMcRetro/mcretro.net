Tillie, Chip and Pudge have arrived at the playground with a rather suspicious plan. Instead of enjoying the equipment like respectable little parrots, they've decided to organise a heist and steal the lot for themselves!

The objective is simple: acquire all the play equipment and never have to share it again. Unfortunately, there are three birds involved in this criminal enterprise, and whether they can cooperate long enough to pull it off remains to be seen.

Will this be the greatest heist in conure history, or will our feathered criminals turn on one another before the job is done? The trouble with running a criminal organisation is that everyone's a birdbrain!
