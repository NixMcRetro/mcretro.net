After doing hard time for their failed heist, Pudge, Tillie and Chip have finally escaped the slammer! The crime-bird gang is making a break for high ground, with an imaginary tide rolling in behind them to wipe out the villages. What an absolutely ridiculous situation these three have managed to get themselves into!

This is what happens when you let the parrots organise their own criminal enterprise. First they want all the playground equipment, and now they're fugitives on the run. I suppose we'll have to build a better prison next time. Good luck out there, you little criminals!
