A wild Tillie has been spotted causing trouble again! Well, perhaps not so wild, since Tillie has been living here since January. But you wouldn't know it from the amount of cheek this little green conure can fit into a single afternoon.

Chip is nearby for the latest round of hijinks, and I've made a very reasonable request for Tillie to stop being a nuisance. Does Tillie listen? Of course not! The trouble continues, and I'm beginning to wonder what kind of influence this is on our supposedly innocent Chippy. Honestly, these birds!
