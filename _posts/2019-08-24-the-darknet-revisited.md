The darknet, what wonders exist there? Why not check out [retrojunkie.net](https://web.archive.org/web/20190824033151/https://retrojunkie.net/) for your gateway into the lighter side of the darknet! Thanks to a free weekend (or two), I finally got the onion (Tor) side of things running at 100% on Debian Buster too. This time I was using v3 onion services, with their wonderfully enormous 56-character addresses, and picked up some cool vanity onions while I was at it!

Because who doesn't like [sonicmfly4cagomgelwtd4hkbsopuhkahfprcvk5hwvb5i2c2q6s7iqd.onion](https://web.archive.org/web/20180115202632/http://retrojunkie.net/sonic/), [mcretro4lov3ycyvikjdeer3dgl5wel5aer6xakj5mxa26a3qvrp22qd.onion](/) or the classics like [retro56qdosu3zuisr5tiuh7ihpn6owzpwjwvs7djlweiqb3mcspahyd.onion](https://web.archive.org/web/20190824033151/https://retrojunkie.net/) and [frank666vaq7j5kt7ek62urayao3ovmeqr3kiwiulshu32hkseuamuyd.onion](https://web.archive.org/web/20190824025707/http://retrojunkie.net/frank/)

This follows on from [The Darknet, Tor and SSL](/the-darknet-tor-and-ssl/), where I had still been tinkering with the old v2 onion format. Progress!

Pretty neat overall. Helped me tidy up my vhosts too, they were a mess! And got my WordPress hooks fixed up for onion-ness. Hopefully we'll have some videos up in the not too distant future too.
