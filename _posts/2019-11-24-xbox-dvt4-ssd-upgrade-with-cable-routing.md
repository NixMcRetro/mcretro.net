For the 120GB Western Digital SSD upgrade, I moved to an 80-conductor, 40-pin ATA cable to reduce the likelihood of transfer errors. I opted for a 24" version because I wasn't sure whether there'd be any difference in the innards between the DVT4 and my Kasumi Blue retail Xbox. It does go beyond the usual 18" PATA cable length, though. Turns out the cable routing is a little wonky. The good news is we get to see the entire process above!
