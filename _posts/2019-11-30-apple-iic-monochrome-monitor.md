The glorious Apple //c monochrome monitor. Compact. Solid. Heavy. Beefcake. These are the words used to describe this beast. We go ahead and adjust some settings on the yoke with a near-enough, good-enough mentality. We're not perfectionists here - that's for sure!

The near-enough, good-enough attitude applies to the geometry, not the safety. This is a CRT monitor, so there are hazardous high voltages inside and parts can retain charge after power is removed. Yoke adjustment is not something to copy casually if you do not know your way around CRT servicing.
