Big Fish is back! On the 24th October 2019, we've got another look at Trevor, our Australian eel-tailed catfish (*Tandanus tandanus*). What a fish! Trevor had a rather wonderful habit of getting my attention, and I was more than happy to return the favour with the camera. There's something rather endearing about a catfish that seems just as interested in what's happening outside the aquarium as what's going on inside it.

{% include youtube.html id="nALuq2maHvo" %}

The next day, Big Fish is hiding! Well, that's what we're looking for, anyway. One of the entertaining things about keeping catfish is discovering that a fish can be somewhere inside an aquarium while apparently being nowhere in particular.

{% include youtube.html id="DFhV3dwHWZ4" %}

About one month later, on the 20th November 2019, we've got an extended helping of Big Fish! Sometimes a longer recording is the best way to appreciate these animals without rushing through everything. There's no shortage of things to watch when you've got a friendly, curious catfish going about the day.

There was a serious side to all this too. Trevor was living in a 75-litre AquaStyle 510 aquarium, and keeping up with water conditions was becoming increasingly important as Big Fish grew. My notes from the 11th November 2019 recorded 0.25 ppm ammonium (NH4+), zero nitrite and nitrate between 40 and 80 ppm.

{% include youtube.html id="Wyax_pYHkmU" %}

By the 8th December 2019, we're back to watching Trevor swimming around. I could spend ages observing Big Fish! The way an eel-tailed catfish moves through the water is fascinating, and Trevor was certainly a character worth keeping an eye on. There's a reason I kept coming back with the camera.

{% include youtube.html id="jCsqw6LiRvY" %}

And on the 16th December 2019, it's time for Big Fish swim training! Swim training? For a fish? I don't recall issuing any swimming certificates, but I suspect Trevor would have passed with flying colours. For Trevor's earlier adventures and the bigger story behind the aquarium, have a look at [Big Fish (Trevor) the Eel-tailed Catfish!](/trevor-the-eel-tailed-catfish/).

Fish rock!
