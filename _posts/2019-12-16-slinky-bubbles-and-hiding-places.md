Slinky is back! On the 10th November 2019, we've got the resident Senegal bichir (*Polypterus senegalus*) breathing underwater, just one of the two ways to breathe! Yes, actual lungs, as well as gills. I know I've mentioned this before, but how could I not? Most people don't expect their aquarium fish to need a trip to the surface for a breath. Slinky, of course, isn't most aquarium fish.

{% include youtube.html id="vh3Thlnc5vI" %}

A week later, it's time for some Slinky swimming! Between the long body, the little separated dorsal fins and those rather distinctive movements, there's plenty to admire. I don't think I'll ever get tired of the tiny finlets along the back. A whole row of fins! Who came up with that idea?

{% include youtube.html id="Qnk2pimVM4o" %}

Then we hit the 26th November 2019 and it's time for some Slinky versus bubbles! We also have my original [Slinky the Fish](/slinky-the-fish/) article from the same day, because apparently one helping of Slinky simply wasn't enough. I could watch this little fish zoom about for ages!

{% include youtube.html id="HkKidtIecEA" %}

On the 5th December 2019, Slinky gets some new hiding plants! That's an important part of the aquarium too. A fish should have somewhere to retreat when it's had enough of everybody staring through the glass. Especially when everybody includes me and a camera! The tank isn't just something for us to look at. It's where the fish live.

{% include youtube.html id="04RSOuMqmR8" %}

By the 16th December 2019, we've got Slinky hiding away again! Apparently it's back to the great aquarium game of trying to find Slinky. With a whole collection of names including Wiggles, Lizardfish and Night Dragon, you might think one of them would be easier to spot. But Slinky was always the name I liked best.

{% include youtube.html id="CNTVqMXUUaM" %}

And from the very same day, Slinky is back with the bubbles! Hiding places one moment, bubble adventures the next. That's a pretty good collection of Slinky recordings for November and December, and it follows on nicely from the [October Slinky adventures](/slinky-the-senegal-bichir/).
