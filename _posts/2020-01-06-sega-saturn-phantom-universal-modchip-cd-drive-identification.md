This week we have a bunch of Phantom Universal videos which were recorded way back in 2016. The Phantom sits between the Saturn motherboard and CD-ROM drive and handles the disc-authentication check, allowing compatible CD-R media to boot; it does not remove the console's separate region lock. By the time I finally uploaded this footage in 2020, optical-drive replacements such as Rhea were already changing the landscape, with the Satiator also on the way.
