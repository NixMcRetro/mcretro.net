This one went into a Victor V-Saturn that had recently been unmodded. We've modded it with a much newer Phantom Universal created by Bad_Ad84 of the now-defunct ASSEMblergames.com. The Phantom handles the Saturn's disc-authentication check so CD-R backups can boot, but it does not remove the separate region lock. Imports still need their own region-bypass method, such as an Action Replay or a region-free BIOS setup. Pseudo Saturn Kai can handle both region bypass and CD-R booting from a compatible cartridge, which was already making an internal modchip less essential for a lot of setups.
