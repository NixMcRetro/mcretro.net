I knew I'd eventually get around to setting up some sort of gaming live stream!

![](/assets/images/2020/img_0651.jpg)

Too bad OBS on my Mac doesn't run terribly well and tends to crash when in the background. Yet when booted to Windows 10, it's perfect! As a result I'm on the hunt for a small form factor streaming PC. I figured I just needed a bunch of CPU cores to handle processing. That's useful for software encoding, but a supported hardware encoder can take much of that load off the CPU.

{% include youtube.html id="kfr0snfCI64" %}

I had been doing streams via a very cheap $11 composite capture USB stick. It is absolute garbage, but it sure does match what composite was all about - quick and dirty!

{% include youtube.html id="GgYzKOnJhQU" %}

I've now stepped up and have a StarTech USB3HDCAP, but need RGB cables to arrive along with an OSSC (Open Source Scan Converter) I picked up. Of course I buy the revision 1.6 just before they announce the OSSC Pro, but that's alright I suppose! In the grand scheme of things, I just need it to do a better output than composite.

{% include youtube.html id="CLDAlLLiW_0" %}

The future sure is looking interesting, the Dreamcast footage was captured on a faulty capture device I got a full refund for, which was most welcome. I've since passed it on to someone else. Maybe they'll use it, maybe they won't!

{% include youtube.html id="CPolnHIkzeo" %}

All in all, things are looking up! Stay tuned (on the McRetro Gaming channel, not on the Nix McRetro channel).
