When I named my YouTube channel RetroJunkie2020 back on 11th March 2012... Hey! That's almost eight years ago!... I never would have thought the channel would survive that long. It's been with me for longer than most things in my life and I am very thankful for that.

Most of the videos I've been uploading lately are actually from three or four years ago. University took up a fair bit of my time, and now that I'm almost finished, I've gone back to work. Which, as it turns out, isn't quite as boring as I make it sound! There's still plenty of footage from 2016 waiting to see the light of day.

McRetro Gaming has been getting some attention too. That's where I've been experimenting with retro gaming streams, partly so the copyright strikes don't end up on this channel! I've got an OSSC and a DE10-Nano for the MiSTer project, although there are still a few bits missing before I can give the latter a proper workout. And yes, I like scanlines. Sorry if you don't! ;)

I've also moved back to Sydney, where the recent storms have even managed to send rain through my window. Lovely! Between work, old footage, streaming and trying to get all this hardware organised, there's certainly no shortage of things to do.

Thank you, the subscribers and friends of this channel. You are great!
