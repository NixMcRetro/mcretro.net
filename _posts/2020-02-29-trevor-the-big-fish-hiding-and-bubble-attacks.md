Big Fish is back! On the 9th February 2020, we're catching up with Trevor, the Australian eel-tailed catfish (*Tandanus tandanus*). Trevor was one of the friendliest fish I've ever known, and always a pleasure to visit.

{% include youtube.html id="s5i1D4H-Mko" %}

Just over a week later, and Big Fish is avoiding the camera! Trevor had plenty of personality, and I always found it entertaining trying to work out what Big Fish was getting up to.

{% include youtube.html id="PetsVgt1n6c" %}

On the 21st February, it's time for Big Fish to meet the bubbles! Yes, bubbles again! Trevor's aquarium adventures had already supplied quite a collection of entertaining moments, and apparently there was still more to come. I could spend ages watching an eel-tailed catfish swimming about. There's something rather wonderful about these Australian freshwater fish!

{% include youtube.html id="4Y0riQp9N5I" %}

And what better way to finish February than with another Big Fish bubble attack! For the 29th February 2020, we're back with Trevor for another adventure. It's a leap year too, so we even managed to squeeze in an extra day of fish appreciation!
