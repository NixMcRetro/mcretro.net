We're back with Todd the Cod and Slinky on this glorious 11th March 2020! Todd was actually a gudgeon, not a cod, but the nickname was much too good to abandon. Slinky had already featured in plenty of recordings on his own, so it's nice to have Todd sharing the spotlight this time. These two had become familiar faces around the community tank, each with very different personalities.

{% include youtube.html id="4h1ns_FuSAU" %}

Same day and we've got Slinky hunting down a feast! Slinky certainly had no shortage of food-related adventures, although I suspect the promise of dinner was considerably more important to Slinky than the presence of my camera!

{% include youtube.html id="eVqikTE73HI" %}

And on the 20th March, Slinky has apparently been promoted to Destroyer of Worlds! Wiggles, Lizardfish and Night Dragon were already in the running as alternative names, but this might be the most ambitious title yet. For more earlier adventures, have a look at [Slinky, Snacks and General Mayhem!](/slinky-snacks-and-general-mayhem/) and [Slinky the Senegal Bichir!](/slinky-the-senegal-bichir/).
