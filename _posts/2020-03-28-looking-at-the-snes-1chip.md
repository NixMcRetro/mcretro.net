Today we look back on the Super Nintendo 1CHIP console. Relevant at just about any time in history, really! To be fair, this video was already a few years old when I posted it. Today I'm actually adding RGB output to a Japanese Nintendo 64, which is quite straightforward if you've got the right revision. For the simple RGB-amp mod on these Japanese consoles, that means a VDC-NUS or VDC-NUS A chip.

I've opted against the HDMI console mods for now and am instead hedging my bets on MiSTer FPGA, or whatever eventually succeeds it, to keep bringing more of this hardware into the FPGA world. Stay tuned!
