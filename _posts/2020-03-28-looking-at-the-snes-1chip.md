Today we look back on the Super Nintendo 1CHIP console, which is relevant at just about any time in history. To be fair, this video was already a few years old. On the day I posted it I was actually adding RGB output to a Japanese Nintendo 64. The simple RGB-amp approach only works on compatible NTSC boards with a VDC-NUS or VDC-NUS A chip, which is what I meant by having the right revision.

I've opted against the HDMI console mods for now and am instead hedging my bets on MiSTer FPGA, or whatever eventually succeeds it, to keep bringing more of this hardware into the FPGA world. Stay tuned!
