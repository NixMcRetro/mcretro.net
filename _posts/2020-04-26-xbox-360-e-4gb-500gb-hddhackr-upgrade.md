What feels like 500 years ago now, I had an Xbox 360 to play Burnout Revenge. I picked up a brand-new Xbox 360 from my local EB Games... Yeeeep! That's how long ago it was, the darned thing was brand new!

This particular Xbox 360 E was the 4GB model, with its own onboard flash storage. I added a compatible Western Digital Blue 500GB 2.5-inch SATA drive, prepared with HDDHackr and a matching 500GB Xbox 360 security sector so the console would recognise it. That gave me 4GB of onboard storage plus a separate 500GB HDD.
