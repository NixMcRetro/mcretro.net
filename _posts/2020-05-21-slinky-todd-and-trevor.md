We've beamed to 1st May 2020, and Slinky is back for another snack. There was certainly no shortage of reasons for me to visit the community tank, and Slinky's appetite had become a recurring feature of these little aquarium updates!

{% include youtube.html id="fNOubCT8_M8" %}

Ten days later, Todd the Cod is back, this time with friends! Todd had quite the personality and a reputation for getting a little snappy after the lights went out, so I was always interested in what he was getting up to. We'd spent plenty of time with Slinky lately, but it was nice to catch up with Todd and the other friends around the community tank too.

{% include youtube.html id="ias8pDzrlNM" %}

And on the 21st May, we're visiting Trevor the Big Fish for a despeckling update! It's been a little while since the February adventures, and it's good to catch up with our friendly Australian eel-tailed catfish (*Tandanus tandanus*) again. Trevor had already supplied plenty of entertaining moments involving hiding places, bubbles and general Big Fish antics.

There are quite a few more Trevor recordings coming up in June too, so the adventures certainly aren't finished yet! For more of Trevor's earlier appearances, have a look at [Trevor the Big Fish!
