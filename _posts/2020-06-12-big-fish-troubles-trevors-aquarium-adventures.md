Big Fish is back! It's the 10th June 2020, and we're starting off with some Big Fish troubles! After catching up with Trevor in May, June brings quite a few more adventures with our friendly Australian eel-tailed catfish (*Tandanus tandanus*). In fact, there are four Trevor recordings from this particular day alone!

{% include youtube.html id="N7hMSkqKl2Q" %}

And now we've got New Big Fish, Scary New Moves! What a title! Trevor had certainly become a rather familiar face by this point, although that didn't mean every visit to the aquarium went quite as expected. There's apparently plenty more Big Fish activity to catch up on today.

{% include youtube.html id="g060bB-wqMs" %}

Next up, bubble hunting! Trevor and bubbles were becoming something of a recurring combination, weren't they? We had the bubble attacks in February, and now we're back with another helping in June. Apparently the bubbles weren't going to get a day off!

{% include youtube.html id="w-P6-W-uGAc" %}

And to finish off the 10th June, the lights are on and Big Fish is home! That's four Trevor recordings from the same day, covering everything from trouble and scary new moves to bubble hunting. Quite a busy day in the Big Fish department!

{% include youtube.html id="0SbRwq4v_Rw" %}

On the 11th June, we're back for a bit of swimming! No particularly elaborate title this time, just Big Fish swimming around. I always enjoyed the opportunity to spend some time with Trevor, whether there was something unusual happening or simply another chance to see how things were going.

{% include youtube.html id="36dZvadT2Ak" %}

The 12th June rolls around and Trevor is playing! What exactly constitutes playtime for an eel-tailed catfish? Well, Trevor certainly provided plenty of opportunities to wonder about that over the years. There's a reason I kept coming back with the camera!

{% include youtube.html id="t6FFE7ciKnY" %}

And on the very same day, it's time for Trevor's Snacky Cakes! Between all that swimming and playing, there's another food-related recording to finish off this little run of Big Fish adventures. For the previous instalment, have a look at [Slinky, Todd and Trevor!](/slinky-todd-and-trevor/)
