This continues the Phantom Universal adventures from earlier in the year. For this installation I used Kynar wire, with 26 to 30 AWG working well for my wiring, including the 5V connection. That's what I used on this build rather than a special wiring requirement of the Phantom itself.

Most of this footage is quite, let's say... aged. By June 2020 there were already newer ways to achieve similar disc-free results, including Rhea / Phoebe, with the Satiator on the way and Fenrir then available for 20-pin drives.

I've also ordered a bunch of capacitors to recap a VA15 This is COOL Saturn. Strangely, they're all through-hole capacitors, and the late VA15 board has both of the Saturn's SH-2 CPUs integrated into a single 315-6018 package rather than the two separate SH-2 packages used on earlier revisions. Anyway, as always enjoy the video and I'll catch you in the next one! :)
