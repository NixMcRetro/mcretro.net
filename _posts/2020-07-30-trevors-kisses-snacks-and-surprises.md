The Big Fish adventures continue! It's the 13th June 2020, and we're back with Trevor for another visit. We've already had a rather busy few days in the aquarium, and there's still plenty more footage to get through. Trevor certainly wasn't short of appearances this month!

{% include youtube.html id="V2qjjUSvvYE" %}

Still the 13th June, and now we've got Big Fish Angry Kisses! Angry kisses? From a catfish? What a wonderful combination! Trevor was a particularly friendly fish, although I suppose even friendly fish can have moments that inspire rather dramatic video titles!

{% include youtube.html id="jujqM08wfHI" %}

The following day, it's time for Trevor to show us some gum! Yes, gum! Our eel-tailed catfish friend has apparently found yet another way to end up in front of the camera. From angry kisses to a close encounter with Big Fish's mouth, June is shaping up to be quite a month.

{% include youtube.html id="iZVtGK14fxY" %}

And here's Trevor again on the 14th June, this time as the most peaceful of Big Fish! A rather different title from yesterday's angry kisses, isn't it? But that's part of what I enjoyed about spending time with Trevor. There was always something worth stopping to appreciate.

{% include youtube.html id="RQA9iZ3D4i4" %}

Fast forward to the 27th June and Big Fish is rejecting food! After all those Snacky Cakes earlier in the month, apparently this particular feeding attempt didn't go to plan. One refused meal doesn't tell us very much on its own, but I thought the recording was worth including alongside the rest of Trevor's June adventures.

{% include youtube.html id="30JyhFNpWDY" %}

Now we've landed on Saturday, the 4th July 2020, and Trevor is having a lazy Saturday! After the busy month of June, perhaps a quieter day was just what Big Fish had in mind. Sometimes it's nice to visit a friend without anything particularly dramatic going on.

{% include youtube.html id="h_1-Oa7IY10" %}

And on the 30th July, we're back for another feeding adventure. Catch the pellet, Big Fish! Trevor was always such a pleasure to spend time with, and this makes a nice addition to the growing collection of recordings from 2020.
