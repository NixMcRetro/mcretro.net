The Big Fish adventures continue! It's the 13th June 2020, and we're back with Trevor for another visit. We've already had a rather busy few days in the aquarium, and there's still plenty more footage to get through. Trevor certainly wasn't short of appearances this month!

{% include youtube.html id="V2qjjUSvvYE" %}

It's still the 13th June, perhaps just an unlucky day. Those kisses don't look happy? Could it be tank size related? Something in the diet that's not right? Water measurements out of control? I am not sure.

{% include youtube.html id="jujqM08wfHI" %}

The following day, it's time for Trevor to show us some gum! Yes, gum! Our eel-tailed catfish friend has apparently found yet another way to end up in front of the camera. From unhappy kisses to a close encounter with being eaten, June is shaping up to be quite a month.

{% include youtube.html id="iZVtGK14fxY" %}

Big Fish is here with a message of peace and love, a far cry from yesterday's unhappy kisses, isn't it? But that's part of what I enjoyed about spending time with Trevor. There was always something worth stopping to appreciate.

{% include youtube.html id="RQA9iZ3D4i4" %}

Fast forward to the 27th June and Big Fish is rejecting food! After all those snacky cakes earlier in the month, apparently this particular feeding attempt didn't go to plan. One refused meal doesn't tell us very much on its own, but I thought the recording was worth including alongside the rest of Trevor's June adventures.

{% include youtube.html id="30JyhFNpWDY" %}

Now we've landed on the 4th July 2020, and Trevor is having a lazy Saturday! After the busy month of June, perhaps a quieter day was just what Big Fish had in mind. Sometimes it's nice to visit a friend without anything particularly dramatic going on.

{% include youtube.html id="h_1-Oa7IY10" %}

And on the 30th July, we're back for another feeding adventure. Catch the pellet, Big Fish! Trevor was always such a pleasure to spend time with!
