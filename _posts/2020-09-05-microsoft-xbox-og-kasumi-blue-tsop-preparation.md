Oh my lovely Kasumi Blue, we have long since parted ways but fond memories still remain. In this video we have another peek inside it and prepare the motherboard for TSOP flashing. This is the same Kasumi Blue in which I identified a v1.0 motherboard despite the much later limited-edition shell. I still don't have evidence showing whether Microsoft built it that way or somebody swapped the motherboard before I got it, so the v1.0 board is the observation and the factory history remains unknown.

A v1.0 motherboard has a flashable onboard TSOP, unlike the later v1.6 hardware. I also swapped in a power supply compatible with this motherboard revision and Australian mains rather than treating arbitrary Xbox PSUs as interchangeable. I still despise step-up/down transformers.
