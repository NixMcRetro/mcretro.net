Oh my lovely Kasumi Blue, we have long since parted ways but fond memories still remain. In this video we have another peek inside it and prepare the motherboard for TSOP flashing. This one had a v1.0 motherboard inside that much later limited-edition shell. Whether it left Microsoft that way or somebody swapped the board before I got it, I don't know! The v1.0 board has a flashable onboard TSOP, unlike the later v1.6 hardware. I also swapped in a power supply compatible with this board and Australian mains.
