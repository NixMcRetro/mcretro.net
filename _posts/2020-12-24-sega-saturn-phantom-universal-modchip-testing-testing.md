What a week it has been! There's always more video of the Phantom Universal from Bad_Ad84 and Zer0-2k11! Here we have a look at it in action with the different media I had on hand and see how the installation behaves.

First up, a retail PAL copy of Nights into Dreams to make sure the Saturn could still read a normal disc. That worked, and so did my backup of Burning Rangers. Always nice when the laser decides to cooperate! It certainly makes testing a modchip considerably easier.

Then I tried an NTSC-U copy of Street Fighter: The Movie. As expected, the Saturn rejected it as unsuitable for the system. With a GameShark running as an Action Replay, I could bypass the region check and get the game booting. The Phantom handles the disc authentication side of things, but the region restriction was another little hurdle to get past.

I hope everyone out there has the best Christmas they can and we'll have a collection overview in the next video. Maybe a live stream of some retro gaming tomorrow though, an excellent way to say Hello Christmas Day 2020!
