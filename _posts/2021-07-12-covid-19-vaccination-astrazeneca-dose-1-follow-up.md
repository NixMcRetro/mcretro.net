Delta is spreading around Sydney, and we've entered another lockdown. I can see why, but if businesses close, how are people meant to pay the bills? I hope the governments work out the financial support soon, because I'm worried about this spreading into every state and territory as people leave NSW.

Here is what happened to me after my first jab of AstraZeneca. I was curious to see how high my temperature would go without paracetamol - I was not disappointed, clocking in a serious fever of 40.9°C. Interestingly there was no headache until the day after, probably because my brain cooked overnight and forgot what pain was. This is a record of what I did, not something to repeat; a fever over 40°C warrants medical advice.
