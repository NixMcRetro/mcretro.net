Today, we're having a look around the internals of the first Mega Drive (HAA-2510), the model 1 VA0. There's an interesting little daughter board installed that rises up from what would have been a single 4-pin oscillator. I guess they found some bugs very late in their testing!

This one is my main display piece, and I was mostly taking it apart to record the capacitor values before giving it a recap. There was plenty of dust in there and a couple of capacitors weren't looking especially cheerful, although I couldn't see any obvious leakage. Some of the chip date codes went right back to 1987, with others from 1988. Good grief, that's a long time ago! There weren't quite as many capacitors as I was expecting either, so perhaps I'd been thinking of another revision.
