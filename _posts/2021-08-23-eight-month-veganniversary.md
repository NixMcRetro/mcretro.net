I've been called a lot of things in my time. Handsome, attractive, portly? But I have never been called a great cook. The above image is one of those fancy non-meat burgers, coupled with fake mayo on a healthy piece of toast. I started to test the waters with the vegan project back in late 2020.

My diet up until this had mainly consisted of chicken and fish (salmon) as primary sources of protein, with salmon becoming a greater part with each passing day. My weight was down to where I wanted it to be with a new goal of stability - something I had previously failed at. So why vegan and not vegetarian? Let's have a look!

**Declining Quality**
[Lilydale](https://lilydalefreerange.com.au) started to sell their free-range chicken at my local supermarket for around twice the price of other leading brands. It tasted great and didn't seem as _formed_ as other chicken. A shift had begun. My regular brand was _not free-range_, which I took to mean caged chickens. That was a mix-up with egg-laying hens: Australian meat chickens are raised in sheds, with free-range birds also having outdoor access. I was still asking what sort of quality of life the animals had. Probably not much, in my view.

I carried on purchasing free-range chicken until that fateful day when I had _that_ chicken katsu curry for lunch. Don't get me wrong. My local go-to for chicken katsu curry was amazing. I loved it... until that day. I'll never forget it. The chicken was _the most_ formed chicken I had ever had the misfortune of tasting. I'd even argue that it wasn't chicken. That was it. I was sick of chicken, one of my core staple foods. My other staple was salmon, but after living with a [big fish](/homepages/fishtankworld), my hunger for salmon was not as strong as it had once been.

**Morals**
After caring for [parrots](https://channelchirp.com/) and [fish](/homepages/fishtankworld), you can see we share a commonality with these animals. My conscience got the better of me. Creating life with endless suffering, forced to exist and then only to be destroyed, sometimes in the most inhumane ways. All living creatures deserve the best quality of life that we can offer. What's the point of being at the top of the evolutionary chain if you cannot protect the ones "beneath" you?

**Health**
Health was not the main driver for this shift. I had never eaten much red meat anyway, mainly due to being a little on the lazy side when it came to cooking and cleaning in the kitchen. Well-planned vegetarian or vegan diets can be healthy and may reduce the risk of some chronic diseases, but vegan isn't a magic health label. I remained a dedicated junk-food vegan: fungus nuggets, fake mayonnaise, plant-based meat patties, and salt and vinegar dusted mixed nuts. You won't catch me eating lettuce on a burger. I'd rather inhale a packet of Oreos. As long as it isn't more work, it's alright with me.

**Old Age**
Maybe this is just what happens when you get older. You start to crave sushi, fake mayonnaise, plant-based burger patties and the like. Or perhaps it is the sum of my experiences and a dear flexitarian friend showing me that other possibilities existed. While I didn't realise it at the time, it seems that maybe they were on to something. In hindsight, I am glad to have been shown that path and am very happy to continue along.
