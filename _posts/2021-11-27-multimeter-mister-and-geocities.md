This video dates back to mid-July 2021. We were in the middle of a pandemic, with the Delta variant doing the rounds. Just today I was surprised to learn that the virus did what viruses do: it mutated some more. As I write this in late November, WHO has just named Omicron a variant of concern. Now we're heading into an Omicron-powered pandemic.

Anyway, this video isn't much about anything. I had been meaning to release it a while back but somehow it kept getting missed. It goes through the motions of explaining my plans for the GeoCities archive and how my MiSTer is configured.

GeoCities did quite well and is up and running. The MiSTer is still running along well, and I've just seen news that Robert Peip's developing PlayStation core can now boot a commercial PlayStation 1 game dump. This was a Patreon beta, with no audio yet, but that's progress! You can view (or skim) the live stream mentioned in the video above... below!
