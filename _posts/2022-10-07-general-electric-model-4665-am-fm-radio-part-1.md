There always needs to be a starting point. And this is where we are starting. An overview of the General Electric Model 4665 AM/FM Electronic Digital Clock Radio. Neat things about this radio is that it has a vacuum fluorescent display (VFD) for the clock section.

![](/assets/images/2022/img_0963.jpg)

VFD has always been an arch-nemesis of [my precious Nixie clock](/nixie-clock-slot-machine-demo-by-chronix/). I don't think the technology was that much of an improvement and quite frankly love the glow of the Nixie tubes. Well that and probably some influence from Steins;Gate, courtesy of SuperNick (Nick). Yes, that Nick.

![](/assets/images/2022/img_0964.jpg)

Overall though, this radio has held up quite well. On closer inspection, the internal date code I found was `8004`, which I read as week 4 of 1980. That fits the era, but without manufacturer documentation I should treat the date-code decoding as an inference rather than a birth certificate. What a way to break in the 80s! I'm sure this jammed out a fair deal of [John Farnham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Farnham) back in the day. Imagine waking up to that every day of the week for 42 years. Pure bliss insanity.
