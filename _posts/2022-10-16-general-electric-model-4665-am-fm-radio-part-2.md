What an incredible find. Around the 11-minute mark I tune into something that actually made me laugh. It's been a while since I've listened to commercial radio, let alone on an analogue radio!

AM was no problem, but FM was making me work for it. I could hear FM stations coming through as I fiddled with the switch, so I started wondering if the contacts were just full of dust and gunk. That switch is looking rather suspicious!

I'd only meant to spend a couple of minutes tuning around, and here I was nearly twelve minutes later! Please enjoy the static noises and, as always, stay **_tuned_** for more to come! 😉
