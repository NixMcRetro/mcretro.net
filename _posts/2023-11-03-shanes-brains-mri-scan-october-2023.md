The latest update in a series of updates it seems! Recently I had the fun of being injected with a gadolinium-based MRI contrast agent. The gadolinium is bound in a chelate; once injected it changes the magnetic properties of nearby water and can make blood vessels and some tissues or lesions stand out more clearly on MRI. Christmas tree effect, but with physics!

![](/assets/images/2023/img_1186.jpg)

Unfortunately we are approaching two months since this headache started and it is still there. I was still taking indometacin 50mg once daily. Some days I increased it to 75mg in one dose. That was me tinkering with a prescription rather than a dosing recommendation. The Australian consumer information specifically says not to use more than the recommended dose or stop the medicine unless your doctor tells you to. It had until then been holding the headache fairly well with one dose each day.
