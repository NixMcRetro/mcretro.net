It's been a rather busy few weeks, although I'm not entirely sure where all the time has gone. Between computers, university, medical appointments and my ever-growing collection of unfinished projects, there's been plenty happening. Apparently I still haven't worked out how to do one thing at a time! :D

McRetro.net has been getting a ridiculous amount of attention lately. I've been restoring old articles, digging through YouTube recordings, fixing links and improving photographs. I've also been tinkering with [Dial-a-Homepage](https://mcretro.net/homepages/modem/), trying to recreate the delightful screaming noises of a dial-up modem, and experimenting with [VRAMpage MD](https://mcretro.net/homepages/vrampage-md/). More recently I've been looking at clustering Macs together with oMLX to run larger AI models. Because apparently one computer isn't enough anymore.

On the personal side, I've now reached **three years on estradiol!** I recently had another set of pellets implanted in my hip, something I've been doing for a few years now. I'm also considering donating some of my hair ahead of facial feminisation surgery, hopefully in February, although that might need to be pushed back a few months. After spending so long growing it, cutting a substantial amount off feels a bit strange. Then again, not getting it caught in absolutely everything would be nice!

Medication has been a bit of a juggling act too. I've stopped progesterone after my latest blood test because it was doing dreadful things to my mood. I also tried C100 CBD oil for the first time, which unfortunately didn't seem to do much of anything. I'm trying to reduce my reliance on clonazepam while finding a manageable balance with pregabalin, although that's proving rather difficult.

University hasn't exactly been easy either, but I did manage to get out and see some live jazz recently. It reminded me just how much I love brass instruments, something I hadn't really thought about for quite a while. There's something wonderful about hearing actual musicians performing instead of another collection of questionable noises coming out of my computers.

And finally, summer is nearly here! The first of the big cicadas have started making themselves heard around 8 pm, including green grocers and black princes. It's one of those familiar Australian sounds that tells me the warmer weather isn't far away.

So that's about where things are at the moment. Plenty happening, plenty still to figure out, and probably far too many projects on the go. At least the cicadas seem to have their annual schedule worked out. :)
