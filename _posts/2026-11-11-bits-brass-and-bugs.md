*Somewhere between Macs, AI experiments, jazz and the first cicadas of summer.*
{: .media-caption }

It's been a rather busy few weeks, although I'm not entirely sure where all the time has gone. Between computers, university, medical appointments and my ever-growing collection of unfinished side projects, there's been plenty happening. I still haven't worked out how to do one thing at a time!

This website has been getting a huge amount of attention lately. I've been restoring old articles, digging through YouTube recordings for context in old posts, fixing links, improving photographs and making a few quality-of-life changes around the site. I've also been tinkering with Dial-a-Homepage, trying to recreate the delightful noises of a dial-up modem, and experimenting with VRAMpage MD. More recently I've been looking at clustering Macs together with oMLX to run larger AI models locally. RAM, it's all about the RAM.

I've now reached three years on estradiol! I recently had another set of pellets implanted in my hip, and I've also got to arrange to donate some of my hair ahead of facial feminisation surgery, hopefully in February 2027. That might need to be pushed back a bit. After spending so long growing my hair, cutting a substantial amount off is probably going to feel rather strange. Then again, not getting it caught in everything would be nice! 😅

Medication has been a bit of a juggling act too. I've stopped progesterone after my latest blood test because it was making me feel terrible. I also tried CBD isolate oil for the first time, which unfortunately didn't seem to do much. I'm still hoping to reduce my reliance on clonazepam and diazepam, ideally stopping them completely, while finding a manageable balance with pregabalin. That's proving a little difficult.

University hasn't exactly been easy either, but thanks to a friend at UTS I did manage to see some live jazz. It reminded me just how much I love brass instruments, something I hadn't really thought about for quite a while. There's something wonderful about hearing *actual* musicians performing instead of another collection of questionable noises coming out of my computers. Don't get me wrong, modems are cool, but jazz trump(et)s it!

And summer is nearly here! The first of the big cicadas have started making themselves heard around 8 pm, including Green Grocers (*Cyclochila australasiae*) and Black Princes (*Psaltoda plaga*). It's one of those familiar Australian sounds that tells me the warmer weather isn't far away. Although, judging by some of the days we've had, it's already here!

I've still got far too many projects on the go, and no sign of that changing any time soon. At least the cicadas seem to have their annual schedule worked out! 🙃
