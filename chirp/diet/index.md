  <h1 id="chirp-diet-heading">Our Diet</h1>
  <p>Pudge, Tillie, and Chip enjoyed a varied collection of fresh fruit, vegetables, pellets, seeds, and treats. Here's the food list remembered from the original Channel Chirp site.</p>
  <p>The flock's favourites included banana, blueberries, strawberries, apple, beans, corn, carrots, pellets, seeds, millet, and occasional nuts. Tillie was fond of peanuts, while Chip had a taste for almonds.</p>
  <p>The original site recommended introducing pellet or brand changes gradually, keeping treats in moderation, and always providing fresh water.</p>
  <p><em>This is the flock's historical food diary, not a veterinary feeding guide.</em></p>
