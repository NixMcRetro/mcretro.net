Toga the Saratoga
A living fossil who lives at the University of New South Wales, Australia.
Toga outgrew his tank in 2017 and was moved to UNSW Biological Sciences South Building.
You can watch his movie on YouTube.
More recent video can be viewed at the Fish Tank World YouTube channel.
Thanks to Professor Iain Suthers at UNSW for additional info.
