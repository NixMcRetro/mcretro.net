# News from the GeoCities Rebuild

These are the dated updates from my original 2021-2022 GeoCities frontend. Back then I was trying to make the archive friendly to old operating systems and browsers, particularly Windows 9x, Internet Explorer 5, and Netscape 4.

The updates are preserved as historical notes. References to the old Tor service, archive search, or public hosting describe features of the **former rebuild**, not services operating today.
