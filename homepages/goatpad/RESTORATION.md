# GOATPAD 1337

Historical source: owner-provided July 2024 archive, including an eight-by-eight soundboard and sequencer with 64 original Ogg audio samples. Original HTML, CSS, artwork, JavaScript sound mappings, and MIT licence are preserved.

Modern-browser compatibility: audio is initialised only after a deliberate **Start GOATPAD 1337** click. The 64 archived audio files are decoded in batches, with visible progress and retry messaging; sound pads become available after loading. The sequencer pattern and original audio samples are unchanged.

The restored static application lives at `/homepages/goatpad/` and must be checked in a browser before merging to `main`.
