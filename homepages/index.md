Welcome to the homepage directory! This is where I've collected some of the little websites, projects and experiments scattered around McRetro.net. Some have been around for years, some are considerably newer, and some still need a bit of archaeological excavation before they're ready for visitors.

At least there's something here now!

### Ready to Visit

**🐶 [Pepper the Dog Blog](/homepages/pepper/)**

Hello, I am Pepper the Dog! The original sausage dog homepage, complete with photographs, adventures and entries dating from 2016 to 2018. Woof!

**🎹 [BleepGarden Music Maker 2000](/homepages/op-1/)**

A browser-based music maker originally created for SuperNick. Make some noise with synthesizers, keyboards, rhythms and songs. Bleep, bloop, bleep!

**🎵 [Lunchbeat](/homepages/lunchbeat/)**

An original 1-bit groovebox running in your web browser, with an electronics project behind it. Sometimes the simplest sounds are the most entertaining!

**🎳 [Nix's Cyber Lanes](/homepages/bowling/)**

An old-school bowling league homepage with scores, averages and a leaderboard. Check out those lanes!

### Historical Homepages Awaiting Restoration

The original website files for the following four homepages have been recovered, including their original HTML and supporting images. They're not yet restored on McRetro.net, so I'm leaving the links out until the actual pages are ready.

- **🦔 Sonic the Hedgehog Homepage (1994 to 1997)**: One of my earliest websites, dedicated to Sonic and pals.
- **🪐 Space / Planets Webpage (1996 to 1997)**: A little journey around the Solar System.
- **🐍 Frank the Snake**: A big snake with a big heart and a photo gallery.
- **🌳 Super Nature World**: A little corner of the internet dedicated to nature.

There's more homepage archaeology to do as well, including **Fish Tank World** and **GeoCities**. Those sections still need proper restoration.
