SuperNick's OP-1 Browser Lab · quick guide

This is an independent HTML/Web Audio homage to the original OP-1, not official Teenage Engineering firmware or an exact emulation. Version 0.8 adds verified local recovery checkpoints, safer audio storage, memory cleanup and project integrity checks. Existing projects remain compatible.

  • Play notes: bottom octave Z S X D C V G B H N J M; upper octave Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U. Use the on-screen keys too.
  • Control a sound: select SYNTH, choose sound 1–8, then press screen keys 1–4 to change engine, ADSR, effects or LFO. Drag / scroll the coloured knobs; double-click to reset.
  • Drums: switch to DRUM, select a kit 1–8, then tap different notes for synthesized percussion.
  • Record: choose TAPE, select a track with screen keys 1–4, arm REC, then PLAY. Perform while tape rolls. STOP completes the recording. REC can be armed while the tape is moving to overdub. Clicking REC again disarms it.
  • Tape: left/right transport arrows move four seconds; SHIFT + rewind returns to zero. Use SET IN / SET OUT to mark a loop region, ZOOM to inspect it, and click the screen ruler to seek. LOOP repeats the chosen region.
  • Clear a track: hold SHIFT (or click the SHIFT button), then press ⌫ while in TAPE mode. Clearing a track can now be undone during the current session.
  • Timing: CLICK enables a beat metronome, GRID quantises new note onsets to the nearest sixteenth note, and the tape screen shows musical bar guides.
  • Edit tape: select a note or audio waveform on the TAPE screen, then tap ✂ EDIT on the screen. SPLIT divides it at the playhead, LIFT removes it to a clipboard and DROP places a copy at the playhead on the chosen track. UNDO and REDO protect edits during this session.
  • Synth effects: choose SYNTH and select the on-screen FX tab. The orange knob adjusts drive; hold SHIFT to adjust chorus depth with that knob. All four knobs work on the FX page.
  • Mixer: four knobs change track volumes. Hold SHIFT while changing a knob to pan its corresponding track.
  • Sequencer: ✣ now opens a five-mode picker directly on the OP-1 screen. Select Pattern, Endless, Arpeggio, Finger or Tombola there; the display buttons 1–4 and coloured encoders control the active sequencer. Use ☰ MODES to switch again. Advanced controls remain below the instrument. Choose rate, swing and direction. Press RUN to audition. To record, arm REC and press PLAY.
  • Tape speed: change SPEED to 0.5× through 2× and toggle REVERSE for backwards playback of PCM clips. Note events trigger backwards in time but retain forward envelopes. WAV export respects speed and direction. Microphone and resampling are recorded at 1× to preserve audio integrity.
  • Audio sampling: MIC REC requests microphone permission and records into the selected track while tape runs. RESAMPLE captures the instrument's actual audio mix including any other tracks (and the metronome if enabled). + TAPE AUDIO imports a recording at the playhead. + SAMPLE loads an instrument sample; SAMPLE KEYS plays 4, 8 or 16 sliced pads using the instrument keyboard. Browser storage is used for local audio cache.
  • Save: verified autosave checkpoints reference audio stored in IndexedDB, with a previous checkpoint available for recovery. SAVE PROJECT embeds audio in portable JSON, and EXPORT WAV renders the combined note and audio tracks.

Known limits of v0.8: this is still an independent OP-1-inspired instrument, not a complete emulation. PCM clips are 22.05 kHz mono; waveform operations and sample pads are simplified. Tape supports forward/reverse PCM playback; reverse note events use forward envelopes, and sequencers are independent browser interpretations, not firmware replicas. Full hardware tape tricks/MIDI are not yet implemented. Playback and WAV export synthesize from the saved notes.
