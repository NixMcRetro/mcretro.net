## Mars Bar Slice

*Recipe adapted from Lyndelle Lindner*

![Recipe photograph IMG_9804](/assets/images/2024/04/mars-bar-slice/IMG_9804.jxl)

### Ingredients

- 128 g of Mars Bar
- 2 cups Rice Bubbles
- 60 g butter

### Method

1. Cut Mars bars into pieces.
2. Melt slowly on low heat with butter in a saucepan.
3. When completely melted pour into a mixing bowl with Rice Bubbles.
4. Mix well, probably.
5. Press mixture into a tray.
6. When cooled cut into squares.
7. Eat immediately or store in fridge.
8. Serve cool, but not cold.
