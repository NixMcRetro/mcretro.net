## Quick Mix Chocolate Cake

*Recipe by Sharon Kotzur*

![Quick Mix Chocolate Cake photograph](/assets/images/2024/04/quick-mix-chocolate-cake/placeholder.jxl)

### Cake Base

- 185 g butter
- 185 g castor sugar
- 155 g self-raising flour
- 85 g cocoa
- 3 eggs
- 125 mL milk

### Base Method

Combine all ingredients in a bowl and beat well. Bake at 190-200°C for 30 minutes.

### Fudge Frosting

- 275 g icing sugar
- 60 g dark cooking chocolate
- 15 g butter
- 40 mL milk

### Fudge Method

Melt chocolate and butter over hot water, stir in sifted icing sugar and enough milk to give a thick spreading consistency. Spread over cake base.
